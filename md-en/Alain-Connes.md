**Alain Connes** (French: [\[alɛ̃ kɔn\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/French "Help:IPA/French"); born 1 April 1947) is a French mathematician, known for his contributions to the study of [operator algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_algebra "Operator algebra") and [noncommutative geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noncommutative_geometry "Noncommutative geometry"). He is a professor at the [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coll%C3%A8ge_de_France "Collège de France"), [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_%C3%89tudes_Scientifiques "Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques"), [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University "Ohio State University") and [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University "Vanderbilt University"). He was awarded the [Fields Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fields_Medal "Fields Medal") in 1982.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-1)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:0-2)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:1-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:2-4)

## Career

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Alain_Connes\&action=edit\&section=1 "Edit section: Career")]

Alain Connes attended high school at [Lycée Saint-Charles](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Lyc%C3%A9e_Saint-Charles_\(Marseille\)\&action=edit\&redlink=1 "Lycée Saint-Charles (Marseille) (page does not exist)") \[[fr](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyc%C3%A9e_Saint-Charles_\(Marseille\) "fr:Lycée Saint-Charles (Marseille)")] in [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marseille "Marseille"), and was then a student of the [classes préparatoires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classe_pr%C3%A9paratoire_aux_grandes_%C3%A9coles "Classe préparatoire aux grandes écoles") in [Lycée Thiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Lyc%C3%A9e_Thiers\&action=edit\&redlink=1 "Lycée Thiers (page does not exist)") \[[fr](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyc%C3%A9e_Thiers "fr:Lycée Thiers")]. Between 1966 and 1970 he studied at [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89cole_normale_sup%C3%A9rieure_\(Paris\) "École normale supérieure (Paris)") in [Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris "Paris"), and in 1973 he obtained a PhD from [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University "Pierre and Marie Curie University"), under the supervision of [Jacques Dixmier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Dixmier "Jacques Dixmier").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:0-2)

From 1970 to 1974 he was research fellow at the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research "French National Centre for Scientific Research") and during 1975 he held a visiting position at [Queen's University at Kingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen%27s_University_at_Kingston "Queen's University at Kingston") in Canada.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-Alain_Connes_2021-5)

In 1976 he returned to France and worked as professor at Pierre and Marie Curie University until 1980 and at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research "French National Centre for Scientific Research") between 1981 and 1984. Moreover, since 1979 he holds the Léon Motchane Chair at [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_%C3%89tudes_Scientifiques "Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques").[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-6) From 1984 until his retirement in 2017 he held the chair of Analysis and Geometry at [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coll%C3%A8ge_de_France "Collège de France").[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-Alain_Connes_2021-5)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-7)

In parallel, he was awarded a distinguished professorship at [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University "Vanderbilt University") between 2003 and 2012,[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-8) and at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University "Ohio State University") between 2012 and 2021.

In 2000 he was an invited professor at the [Conservatoire national des arts et métiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservatoire_national_des_arts_et_m%C3%A9tiers "Conservatoire national des arts et métiers").[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-9)

## Research

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Alain_Connes\&action=edit\&section=2 "Edit section: Research")]

Connes' main research interests revolved around [operator algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_algebra "Operator algebra"). Besides [noncommutative geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noncommutative_geometry "Noncommutative geometry"), he has applied his works in various areas of mathematics and [theoretical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_physics "Theoretical physics"), including [number theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_theory "Number theory"), [differential geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_geometry "Differential geometry") and [particle physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_physics "Particle physics").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:0-2)[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-10)[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:1-3)[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:2-4) Since the 1990s, he has been a proponent of [the spectral Standard Model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noncommutative_standard_model "Noncommutative standard model"), which gives quantum behaviour to positions in spacetime, sidestepping some of the problems with [quantum gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_gravity "Quantum gravity").[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-11)

In his early work on [von Neumann algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_algebra "Von Neumann algebra") in the 1970s, he succeeded in obtaining the almost complete classification of injective [factors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_algebra#Factors "Von Neumann algebra"). He also formulated the [Connes embedding problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connes_embedding_problem "Connes embedding problem").

Following this, he made contributions in [operator K-theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-theory "K-theory") and [index theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_theory "Index theory"), which culminated in the [Baum–Connes conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baum%E2%80%93Connes_conjecture "Baum–Connes conjecture"). He also introduced [cyclic cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_homology "Cyclic homology") in the early 1980s as a first step in the study of [noncommutative differential geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noncommutative_geometry "Noncommutative geometry").[\[12\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-12)[\[13\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-13)[\[14\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-14)[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-15)

He was a member of [Nicolas Bourbaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Bourbaki "Nicolas Bourbaki").[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-16) Over many years, he collaborated extensively with [Henri Moscovici](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Moscovici "Henri Moscovici").[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:0-2)

## Awards and honours

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Alain_Connes\&action=edit\&section=3 "Edit section: Awards and honours")]

Connes was awarded the [Peccot-Vimont Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peccot_Lectures "Peccot Lectures") in 1976,[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-17) the [Ampère Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amp%C3%A8re_Prize "Ampère Prize") in 1980, the [Fields Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fields_Medal "Fields Medal") in 1982,[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FieldsMedal00-18)[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FieldsMedal01-19) the [Clay Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay_Research_Award "Clay Research Award") in 2000[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-20) and the [Crafoord Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crafoord_Prize "Crafoord Prize") in 2001.[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-21)[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-CrafoordPrize00-22)[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-23) The [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS "CNRS") granted him the [silver medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Silver_Medal "CNRS Silver Medal") in 1977[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-24) and the [gold medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Gold_Medal "CNRS Gold Medal") in 2004.[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-25)[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-26)[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-27)[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-28)[\[29\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-29)

He was an [invited speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians "Invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians") at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congress_of_Mathematicians "International Congress of Mathematicians") in 1974 at Vancouver and in 1986 at Berkeley, and a plenary speaker at the ICM in 1978 at Helsinki.

## Family

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Alain_Connes\&action=edit\&section=4 "Edit section: Family")]

Alain Connes is the middle-born of three sons — born to parents both of whom lived to be 101 years old. He married in 1971.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:0-2)
