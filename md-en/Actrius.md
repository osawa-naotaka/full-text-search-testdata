***Actresses*** ([Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language "Catalan language"): ***Actrius***) is a 1997 [Catalan language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language "Catalan language") Spanish drama film produced and directed by [Ventura Pons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ventura_Pons "Ventura Pons") and based on the award-winning stage play *E.R.* by [Josep Maria Benet i Jornet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josep_Maria_Benet_i_Jornet "Josep Maria Benet i Jornet"). The film has no male actors, with all roles played by females.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-El_Pais-1) The film was produced in 1996. [\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-2)

## Synopsis

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Actrius\&action=edit\&section=1 "Edit section: Synopsis")]

In order to prepare herself to play a role commemorating the life of legendary actress Empar Ribera, young actress ([Mercè Pons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merc%C3%A8_Pons "Mercè Pons")) interviews three established actresses who had been the Ribera's pupils: the international diva Glòria Marc ([Núria Espert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%BAria_Espert "Núria Espert")), the television star Assumpta Roca ([Rosa Maria Sardà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosa_Maria_Sard%C3%A0 "Rosa Maria Sardà")), and dubbing director Maria Caminal ([Anna Lizaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Lizaran "Anna Lizaran")).[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-SFF-3)

## Recognition

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Actrius\&action=edit\&section=3 "Edit section: Recognition")]

### Screenings

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Actrius\&action=edit\&section=4 "Edit section: Screenings")]

*Actrius* screened in 2001 at the [Grauman's Egyptian Theatre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grauman%27s_Egyptian_Theatre "Grauman's Egyptian Theatre") in an [American Cinematheque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Cinematheque "American Cinematheque") retrospective of the works of its director. The film had first screened at the same location in 1998.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-LA_Times-4) It was also shown at the 1997 [Stockholm International Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_International_Film_Festival "Stockholm International Film Festival").[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-SFF-3)

### Reception

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Actrius\&action=edit\&section=5 "Edit section: Reception")]

In *Movie - Film - Review*, Christopher Tookey wrote that though the actresses were "competent in roles that may have some reference to their own careers", the film "is visually unimaginative, never escapes its stage origins, and is almost totally lacking in revelation or surprising incident".[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-Tookey-5) Noting that there were "occasional, refreshing moments of intergenerational bitchiness", they did not "justify comparisons to *[All About Eve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_About_Eve "All About Eve")*", and were "insufficiently different to deserve critical parallels with *[Rashomon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashomon "Rashomon")*".[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-Tookey-5) He also wrote that *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian "The Guardian")* called the film a "slow, stuffy chamber-piece", and that *[The Evening Standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Evening_Standard "The Evening Standard")* stated the film's "best moments exhibit the bitchy tantrums seething beneath the threesome's composed veneers".[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-Tookey-5) [MRQE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MRQE "MRQE") wrote "This cinematic adaptation of a theatrical work is true to the original, but does not stray far from a theatrical rendering of the story."[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-MRQE-6)

### Awards and nominations

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Actrius\&action=edit\&section=6 "Edit section: Awards and nominations")]

- 1997, won 'Best Catalan Film' at [Butaca Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butaca_Awards "Butaca Awards") for [Ventura Pons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ventura_Pons "Ventura Pons")
- 1997, won 'Best Catalan Film Actress' at Butaca Awards, shared by [Núria Espert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N%C3%BAria_Espert "Núria Espert"), [Rosa Maria Sardà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosa_Maria_Sard%C3%A0 "Rosa Maria Sardà"), [Anna Lizaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Lizaran "Anna Lizaran"), and [Mercè Pons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merc%C3%A8_Pons "Mercè Pons")
- 1998, nominated for 'Best Screenplay' at [Goya Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goya_Awards "Goya Awards"), shared by [Josep Maria Benet i Jornet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josep_Maria_Benet_i_Jornet "Josep Maria Benet i Jornet") and Ventura Pons
