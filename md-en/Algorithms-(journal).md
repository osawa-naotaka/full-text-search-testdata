***Algorithms*** is a monthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed "Peer-reviewed") [open-access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-access "Open-access") [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal "Scientific journal") of [mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics "Mathematics"), covering design, analysis, and experiments on [algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithm "Algorithm"). The journal is published by [MDPI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MDPI "MDPI") and was established in 2008. The founding [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief "Editor-in-chief") was [Kazuo Iwama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazuo_Iwama_\(computer_scientist\) "Kazuo Iwama (computer scientist)") ([Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University "Kyoto University")).[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-1) From May 2014 to September 2019, the editor-in-chief was Henning Fernau ([Universität Trier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universit%C3%A4t_Trier "Universität Trier")). The current editor-in-chief is Frank Werner ([Otto-von-Guericke-Universität Magdeburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto-von-Guericke-Universit%C3%A4t_Magdeburg "Otto-von-Guericke-Universität Magdeburg")).

## Abstracting and indexing

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Algorithms_\(journal\)\&action=edit\&section=1 "Edit section: Abstracting and indexing")]

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports "Journal Citation Reports")*, the journal has a 2022 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor "Impact factor") of 2.3.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-WoS-2) The journal is abstracted and indexed in:

- [Chemical Abstracts Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_Abstracts_Service "Chemical Abstracts Service")[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-CASSI-3)
- [Ei Compendex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ei_Compendex "Ei Compendex")[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-Compendex-4)
- [Emerging Sources Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerging_Sources_Citation_Index "Emerging Sources Citation Index")[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-ISI-5)
- [Inspec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inspec "Inspec")[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-Inspec-6)
- [MathSciNet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MathSciNet "MathSciNet")
- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus "Scopus")[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-Scopus-7)
- [zbMATH Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZbMATH_Open "ZbMATH Open") (2008–2019).[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-MATH-8)
