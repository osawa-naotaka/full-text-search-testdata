***Animalia*** is an illustrated [children's book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Children%27s_literature "Children's literature") by [Graeme Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graeme_Base "Graeme Base"). It was originally published in 1986, followed by a tenth anniversary edition in 1996, and a 25th anniversary edition in 2012. Over four million copies have been sold worldwide.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-1) A special numbered and signed anniversary edition was also published in 1996, with an embossed gold jacket.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-2)

## Synopsis

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Animalia_\(book\)\&action=edit\&section=1 "Edit section: Synopsis")]

*Animalia* is an [alliterative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alliteration "Alliteration") [alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphabet "Alphabet") book and contains twenty-six illustrations, one for each letter of the alphabet. Each illustration features an animal from the animal kingdom (A is for [alligator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alligator "Alligator") and [armadillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armadillo "Armadillo"), B is for [butterfly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butterfly "Butterfly"), C is for [cat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat "Cat"), etc.) along with a short poem utilizing the letter of the page for many of the words. The illustrations contain many other objects beginning with that letter that the reader can try to identify (however, there are not necessarily "a thousand things, or maybe more", as the author states). As an additional challenge, the author has hidden a picture of himself as a child in every picture.

## Related products

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Animalia_\(book\)\&action=edit\&section=2 "Edit section: Related products")]

Julia MacRae Books published an *Animalia* [colouring book](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colouring_book "Colouring book") in 2008.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-3) [H. N. Abrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abrams_Books "Abrams Books") also published a wall calendar colouring book version for children the same year.[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-4)

H. N. Abrams published *The Animalia Wall Frieze*, a fold-out over 26 feet in length, in which the author created new riddles for each letter.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-5)

The Great American Puzzle Factory created a 300-piece jigsaw puzzle based on the book's cover.[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-6)

## Adaptations

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Animalia_\(book\)\&action=edit\&section=3 "Edit section: Adaptations")]

A [television series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animalia_\(TV_series\) "Animalia (TV series)") was also created, based on the book, which airs in [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada "Canada"). [The Australian Children's Television Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Children%27s_Television_Foundation "Australian Children's Television Foundation") released a teaching resource DVD-ROM in 2011 to accompany the TV series with teaching aids for classroom use.[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-7)

In 2010, The Base Factory and AppBooks released Animalia as an application for [iPad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPad "IPad") and [iPhone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPhone "IPhone")/[iPod Touch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPod_Touch "IPod Touch").[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-8)

## Awards

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Animalia_\(book\)\&action=edit\&section=4 "Edit section: Awards")]

*Animalia* won the Young Australian's Best Book Award in 1987 for Best Picture Story Book.[\[9\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-9)

The [Children's Book Council of Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Children%27s_Book_Council_of_Australia "Children's Book Council of Australia") designated *Animalia* a 1987 [Picture Book of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Children%27s_Book_of_the_Year_Award:_Picture_Book "Children's Book of the Year Award: Picture Book"): Honour Book.[\[10\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-10)

Kid's Own Australian Literature Awards named *Animalia* the 1988 Picture Book Winner.[\[11\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-11)
