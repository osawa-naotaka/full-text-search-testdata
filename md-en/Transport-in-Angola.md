## Railways

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Transport_in_Angola\&action=edit\&section=2 "Edit section: Railways")]

Main article: [Rail transport in Angola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rail_transport_in_Angola "Rail transport in Angola")

There are three separate railway lines in [Angola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angola "Angola"):

- [Luanda Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luanda_Railway "Luanda Railway") ([CFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caminhos_de_Ferro_de_Luanda "Caminhos de Ferro de Luanda")) (northern);
- [Benguela Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benguela_Railway "Benguela Railway") ([CFB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caminho_de_Ferro_de_Benguela "Caminho de Ferro de Benguela")) (central), operated by the [Lobito Atlantic Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobito_Atlantic_Railway "Lobito Atlantic Railway") joint venture;
- [Moçâmedes Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mo%C3%A7%C3%A2medes_Railway "Moçâmedes Railway") ([CFM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mo%C3%A7%C3%A2medes_Railway "Moçâmedes Railway")) (southern);

Reconstruction of these three lines began in 2005 and they are now all operational. The [Benguela Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benguela_Railway "Benguela Railway") connects to the [Democratic Republic of the Congo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo "Democratic Republic of the Congo").

## Pipelines

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Transport_in_Angola\&action=edit\&section=4 "Edit section: Pipelines")]

- gas 352 km; liquid petroleum gas 85 km; crude oil 1,065 km (2013)

In April 2012, the Zambian Development Agency (ZDA) and an Angolan company signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) to build a multi-product pipeline from [Lobito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobito "Lobito") to [Lusaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusaka "Lusaka"), [Zambia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zambia "Zambia"), to deliver various refined products to Zambia.[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-1)[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-2)

Angola plans to build an oil refinery in Lobito in the coming years.
