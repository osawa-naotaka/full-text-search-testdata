# Asia Minor (disambiguation)

Look up ***[Asia Minor](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Asia_Minor "wiktionary:Asia Minor")*** in Wiktionary, the free dictionary.

**Asia Minor** is an alternative name for [Anatolia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatolia "Anatolia"), the westernmost protrusion of Asia, comprising the majority of the Republic of Turkey.

**Asia Minor** may also refer to:

- [*Asia Minor* (album)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Minor_\(album\) "Asia Minor (album)"), an album by Jamaican-born jazz trumpeter Dizzy Reece
- ["Asia Minor" (instrumental)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Minor_\(instrumental\) "Asia Minor (instrumental)"), a 1961 instrumental recording by Jimmy Wisner (operating under the name Kokomo)
