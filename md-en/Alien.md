# Alien

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/99/Wiktionary-logo-en-v2.svg/40px-Wiktionary-logo-en-v2.svg.png)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Wiktionary-logo-en-v2.svg)

Look up ***[alien](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/alien "wiktionary:alien")***, ***[aliens](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/aliens "wiktionary:aliens")***, or ***[Alien](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Alien "wiktionary:Alien")*** in Wiktionary, the free dictionary.

**Alien** primarily refers to:

- [Alien (law)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alien_\(law\) "Alien (law)"), a person in a country who is not a national of that country
  - [Enemy alien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enemy_alien "Enemy alien"), the above in times of war

- [Extraterrestrial life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraterrestrial_life "Extraterrestrial life"), life which does not originate from Earth

  - Specifically, a lifeform with [extraterrestrial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraterrestrial_intelligence "Extraterrestrial intelligence")
  - For fictional extraterrestrial life, see [Extraterrestrials in fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraterrestrials_in_fiction "Extraterrestrials in fiction")

- [Introduced species](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Introduced_species "Introduced species"), a species not native to its environment
