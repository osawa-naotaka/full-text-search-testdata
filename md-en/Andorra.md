**Andorra**,[\[e\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-19) officially the **Principality of Andorra**,[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-constitution-2)[\[f\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-20) is a [sovereign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sovereignty "Sovereignty") [landlocked country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landlocked_country "Landlocked country") on the [Iberian Peninsula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberian_Peninsula "Iberian Peninsula"), in the eastern [Pyrenees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrenees "Pyrenees") in Western Europe, [bordered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra%E2%80%93France_border "Andorra–France border") by [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France "France") to the north and [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain "Spain") to [the south](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra%E2%80%93Spain_border "Andorra–Spain border"). Believed to have been created by [Charlemagne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlemagne "Charlemagne"), Andorra was ruled by the [count of Urgell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counts_of_Urgell "Counts of Urgell") until 988, when it was transferred to the [Roman Catholic Diocese of Urgell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Urgell "Roman Catholic Diocese of Urgell"). The present principality was formed by [a charter in 1278](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par%C3%A9age_of_Andorra_1278 "Paréage of Andorra 1278"). It is currently headed by two co-princes: the [Bishop of Urgell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Urgell "Roman Catholic Diocese of Urgell") in [Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalonia "Catalonia"), Spain, and the [president of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_France "President of France"). Its capital and largest city is [Andorra la Vella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_la_Vella "Andorra la Vella").

Andorra is the [sixth-smallest state in Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_microstates "European microstates"), with an area of 468 square kilometres (181 sq mi) and a population of approximately 79,034.[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-UN_WPP-21)[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-UN_WPP_2022-22) The [Andorran people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorran_people "Andorran people") are a [Romance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italic_peoples "Italic peoples") ethnic group closely related to [Catalans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalans "Catalans").[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-Minahan-23) Andorra is the world's [16th-smallest country by land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_dependencies_by_area "List of countries and dependencies by area") and [11th-smallest by population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_dependencies_by_population "List of countries and dependencies by population").[\[18\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-24) Its capital, Andorra la Vella, is the highest capital city in Europe, at an elevation of 1,023 metres (3,356 feet) [above sea level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Above_mean_sea_level "Above mean sea level").[\[19\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-25) The official language is [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language "Catalan language"), but [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_language "Spanish language"), [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language "Portuguese language"), and [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language "French language") are also commonly spoken.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-cia-3)[\[20\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-26)

[Tourism in Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_Andorra "Tourism in Andorra") brings approximately 8 million visitors to the country annually.[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-es2-27) Andorra is not a member state of the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union "European Union"). It has been a member of the [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations "United Nations") since 1993.[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-28)

## Etymology

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=1 "Edit section: Etymology")]

The origin of the word Andorra is unknown, although several hypotheses have been proposed. The oldest is one put forward by the [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greece "Ancient Greece") historian [Polybius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polybius "Polybius") (*[Histories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Histories_\(Polybius\) "Histories (Polybius)")* III, 35, 1), who describes the Andosins, an [Iberian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberians "Iberians") [Pre-Roman tribe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-Roman_peoples_of_the_Iberian_Peninsula "Pre-Roman peoples of the Iberian Peninsula"), as historically located in the valleys of Andorra and facing the [Carthaginian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Carthage "Ancient Carthage") army in its passage through the Pyrenees during the [Punic Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punic_Wars "Punic Wars"). The word Andosini or Andosins (Ἀνδοσίνοι) may derive from the [Basque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basque_language "Basque language") *handia*, meaning "big" or "giant".[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-Diccionari-29) The Andorran [toponymy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toponymy "Toponymy") shows evidence of Basque language in the area. Another theory suggests that the word Andorra may derive from the old word Anorra that contains the Basque word *ur* ("water").[\[24\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-30)

Another theory suggests that Andorra may derive from [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic "Arabic") *ad-dārra* (الدَّارَة), indicating a vast land which is located among mountains or a thickly wooded place[\[25\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-31) (with *ad-* being the [definite article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Definite_article "Definite article")). When the [Moors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moors "Moors") conquered the Iberian Peninsula, the valleys of the High Pyrenees were covered by large tracts of forest. These regions were not administered by [Muslims](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslims "Muslims"), because of the geographic difficulty of direct rule.[\[26\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-32)

Other theories suggest that the term derives from the [Navarro-Aragonese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navarro-Aragonese "Navarro-Aragonese") "andurrial", which means "land covered with bushes" or "scrubland".[\[27\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-33)

The [folk etymology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_etymology "Folk etymology") holds that Charlemagne had named the region as a reference to the [Biblical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bible "Bible") [Canaanite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canaan "Canaan") valley of [Endor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endor_\(village\) "Endor (village)") or Andor (where the [Midianites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midianites "Midianites") had been defeated), a name bestowed by his heir and son [Louis the Pious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_the_Pious "Louis the Pious") after defeating the Moors in the "wild valleys of Hell".[\[28\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-34)

### Prehistory

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=3 "Edit section: Prehistory")]

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/74/Gravats_del_Roc_de_les_Bruixes.JPG/220px-Gravats_del_Roc_de_les_Bruixes.JPG)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Gravats_del_Roc_de_les_Bruixes.JPG)

Roc de les Bruixes prehistorical sanctuary in [Canillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canillo "Canillo") (detail)

La Balma de la [Margineda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Margineda "La Margineda"), found by archaeologists at [Sant Julià de Lòria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant_Juli%C3%A0_de_L%C3%B2ria "Sant Julià de Lòria"), was settled in 9,500 BCE as a passing place between the two sides of the Pyrenees. The seasonal camp was perfectly located for hunting and fishing by the groups of hunter-gatherers from [Ariege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari%C3%A8ge_\(river\) "Ariège (river)") and [Segre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segre_\(river\) "Segre (river)").[\[29\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-35)

During the [Neolithic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neolithic "Neolithic"), a group of people moved to the [Valley of Madriu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madriu-Perafita-Claror_Valley "Madriu-Perafita-Claror Valley") (the present-day Natural Park located in [Escaldes-Engordany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escaldes%E2%80%93Engordany "Escaldes–Engordany") declared [UNESCO World Heritage Site](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO_World_Heritage_Site "UNESCO World Heritage Site")) as a permanent camp in 6640 BCE. The population of the valley grew cereals, raised domestic livestock, and developed commercial trade with people from [Ségre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A8gre_\(department\) "Sègre (department)") and [Occitania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occitania "Occitania").[\[30\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200932,_33-36)[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix200944_a_92-37)

Other archaeological deposits include the Tombs of [Segudet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segudet "Segudet") ([Ordino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordino "Ordino")) and Feixa del Moro (Sant Julià de Lòria), both dated in 4900–4300 BCE as an example of the [Urn culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urnfield_culture "Urnfield culture") in Andorra.[\[30\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200932,_33-36)[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix200944_a_92-37) The model of small settlements began to evolve to complex urbanism during the [Bronze Age](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronze_Age "Bronze Age"). [Metallurgical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallurgy "Metallurgy") items of iron, ancient coins, and reliquaries can be found in the [ancient sanctuaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanctuary "Sanctuary") scattered around the country.

The sanctuary of Roc de les Bruixes (Stone of the Witches) is perhaps the most important archeological complex of this age in Andorra, located in the parish of [Canillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canillo "Canillo"), about the rituals of funerals, ancient scripture and engraved stone [murals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mural "Mural").[\[32\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200934,_35,_38,_39-38)[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix200944_a_92-37)

### Iberian and Roman Andorra

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=4 "Edit section: Iberian and Roman Andorra")]

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/27/Ann%C3%ADbal%2C_andosins.png/220px-Ann%C3%ADbal%2C_andosins.png)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Ann%C3%ADbal,_andosins.png)

[Hannibal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannibal "Hannibal")'s route (red) during the [Second Punic War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Punic_War "Second Punic War"). The Iberian tribes (green) fought against the [Carthaginian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Carthage "Ancient Carthage") army in the Pyrenees.

The inhabitants of the valleys were traditionally associated with the [Iberians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberians "Iberians") and historically located in Andorra as the Iberian tribe Andosins or Andosini (Ἀνδοσίνους) during the 7th and 2nd centuries BC. Influenced by the [Aquitanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquitanian_language "Aquitanian language"), [Basque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basque_language "Basque language") and [Iberian languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberian_languages "Iberian languages"), the locals developed some current toponyms. Early writings and documents relating to this group of people goes back to the second century BC by the Greek writer [Polybius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polybius "Polybius") in his *Histories* during the [Punic Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punic_Wars "Punic Wars").[\[33\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-39)[\[34\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200943-40)[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix200944_a_92-37)[\[35\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200936,_37-41)

Some of the most significant remains of this era are the Castle of the Roc d'Enclar (part of the early [Marca Hispanica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marca_Hispanica "Marca Hispanica")),[\[36\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200944,_45,_46,_47-42) l'Anxiu in [Les Escaldes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Escaldes "Les Escaldes") and Roc de L'Oral in [Encamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encamp "Encamp").[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix200944_a_92-37)[\[35\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200936,_37-41)

The presence of [Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Empire "Roman Empire") influence is recorded from the 2nd century BCE to the 5th century CE. The places with the most Roman presence are in Camp Vermell (Red Field) in [Sant Julià de Lòria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant_Juli%C3%A0_de_L%C3%B2ria "Sant Julià de Lòria"), and in some places in Encamp and in the Roc d'Enclar. People continued trading, mainly with wine and cereals, with the Roman cities of [Urgellet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urgellet "Urgellet") (the present-day [La Seu d'Urgell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Seu_d%27Urgell "La Seu d'Urgell")) and across Segre through the [*via romana*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_roads "Roman roads") Strata Ceretana (also known as Strata Confluetana).[\[31\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix200944_a_92-37)[\[37\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200952,_53-43)[\[36\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200944,_45,_46,_47-42)

### Visigoths and Carolingians: the legend of Charlemagne

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=5 "Edit section: Visigoths and Carolingians: the legend of Charlemagne")]

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/75/Charlemagne_et_Louis_le_Pieux.jpg/220px-Charlemagne_et_Louis_le_Pieux.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Charlemagne_et_Louis_le_Pieux.jpg)

[Charlemagne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlemagne "Charlemagne") instructing his son, [Louis the Pious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_the_Pious "Louis the Pious")

After the [fall of the Roman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_the_Roman_Empire "Fall of the Roman Empire"), Andorra came under the influence of the [Visigoths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visigothic_Kingdom "Visigothic Kingdom"), the [Kingdom of Toledo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Toledo,_Spain#Visigothic_Toledo "History of Toledo, Spain"), and the [Diocese of Urgell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diocese_of_Urgell "Diocese of Urgell"). The Visigoths remained in the valleys for 200 years, during which time [Christianity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_Christianity "Gothic Christianity") spread. When the [Muslim Empire of Al-Andalus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Andalus "Al-Andalus") replaced the ruling Visigoths in most of the Iberian Peninsula, Andorra was sheltered from these [Arab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab "Arab") invaders by the [Franks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francia "Francia").[\[38\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009-44)

Tradition holds that Charles the Great (Charlemagne) granted a charter to the Andorran people for a contingent of 5,000 soldiers under the command of Marc Almugaver, in return for fighting against the [Moors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moors "Moors") near [Porté-Puymorens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Port%C3%A9-Puymorens "Porté-Puymorens") ([Cerdanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerdanya "Cerdanya")).[\[39\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-45)

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/72/Parr%C3%B2quies_andorra.png/220px-Parr%C3%B2quies_andorra.png)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Parr%C3%B2quies_andorra.png)

The six old parishes, each named for their [patron saint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patron_saint "Patron saint"), as depicted in the *Acta de Consagració i Dotació de la Catedral de la Seu d'Urgell* (839)

Andorra remained part of the [Frankish *Marca Hispanica*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hispanic_Marches "Hispanic Marches"), the buffer zone between the [Frankish Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francia "Francia") and the Muslim territories, Andorra being part of the territory ruled by the [Count of Urgell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counts_of_Urgell "Counts of Urgell") and eventually the bishop of the Diocese of Urgell. Tradition also holds that it was guaranteed by the son of Charlemagne, [Louis the Pious](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_the_Pious "Louis the Pious"), writing the *Carta de Poblament* or a local [municipal charter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_charter "Municipal charter") c. 805.[\[40\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-46)

In 988, Count [Borrell II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borrell_II "Borrell II") of Urgell gave the Andorran valleys to the Diocese of Urgell in exchange for land in [Cerdanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerdanya "Cerdanya").[\[41\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-histo1-47) Since then, the bishop of Urgell, based in Seu d'Urgell, has been [co-prince](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-Princes_of_Andorra "Co-Princes of Andorra") of Andorra.[\[42\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-elements-48)

The first document that mentions Andorra as a territory is the *Acta de Consagració i Dotació de la Catedral de la Seu d'Urgell* (Deed of Consecration and Endowment of the Cathedral of La Seu d'Urgell). The document, dated 839, depicts the six old [parishes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parishes_of_Andorra "Parishes of Andorra") of the Andorran valleys that made up the country's administrative division.[\[43\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix200996_a_146-49)

### Middle Ages: The Paréages and the founding of the Co-Principality

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=6 "Edit section: Middle Ages: The Paréages and the founding of the Co-Principality")]

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/28/Esgl%C3%A9sia_de_Sant_Joan_de_Caselles_-_7.jpg/220px-Esgl%C3%A9sia_de_Sant_Joan_de_Caselles_-_7.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Esgl%C3%A9sia_de_Sant_Joan_de_Caselles_-_7.jpg)

[Sant Joan de Caselles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant_Joan_de_Caselles "Sant Joan de Caselles") church, dating from the 11th century, part of the Andorran Romanesque heritage

Before 1095, Andorra had no military protection, and the bishop of Urgell, who knew that the count of Urgell wanted to reclaim the Andorran valleys,[\[42\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-elements-48) asked the [Lord of Caboet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lord_of_Caboet "Lord of Caboet") for help and protection. In 1095, the lord of Caboet and the bishop of Urgell signed under oath a declaration of their co-sovereignty over Andorra. [Arnalda de Caboet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnalda_de_Caboet "Arnalda de Caboet"), daughter of Arnau of Caboet, married the viscount of Castellbò. Their daughter, [Ermessenda de Castellbò](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ermessenda_de_Castellb%C3%B2 "Ermessenda de Castellbò"),[\[44\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-50) married the [count of Foix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Count_of_Foix "Count of Foix"), [Roger-Bernard II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger-Bernard_II,_Count_of_Foix "Roger-Bernard II, Count of Foix"). Roger-Bernard II and Ermessenda shared rule over Andorra with the bishop of Urgell.

In the 13th century, a military dispute arose between the bishop of Urgell and the count of Foix as aftermath of the [Cathar Crusade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathar_Crusade "Cathar Crusade"). The conflict was resolved in 1278 with the mediation of the [king of Aragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_Aragon "King of Aragon"), [Peter III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_III_of_Aragon "Peter III of Aragon"), between the bishop and the count, by the signing of the [first paréage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Par%C3%A9age_of_Andorra_1278 "Paréage of Andorra 1278"), which provided that Andorra's sovereignty be shared between the count of Foix[\[42\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-elements-48) (whose title would ultimately transfer to the French head of state) and the bishop of Urgell, in [Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalonia "Catalonia"). This gave the principality its territory and political form.[\[43\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix200996_a_146-49)[\[45\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton2009-51)

A second paréage was signed in 1288 after a dispute when the count of Foix ordered the construction of a castle in Roc d'Enclar.[\[43\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix200996_a_146-49)[\[45\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton2009-51) The document was ratified by the noble notary [Jaume Orig of Puigcerdà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_of_Cerdanya "County of Cerdanya"), and construction of military structures in the country was prohibited.[\[46\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200960,_61-52)[\[43\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix200996_a_146-49)

In 1364, the political organization of the country named the figure of the [syndic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syndic "Syndic") (now spokesman and president of the parliament) as representative of the Andorrans to their co-princes, making possible the creation of [local departments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parishes_of_Andorra "Parishes of Andorra") (comuns, quarts and veïnats). After being ratified by Bishop Francesc Tovia and Count [John I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_I,_Count_of_Foix "John I, Count of Foix"), the Consell de la Terra or [Consell General de les Valls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Council_\(Andorra\) "General Council (Andorra)") (General Council of the Valleys) was founded in 1419, the second oldest parliament in Europe. The syndic Andreu d'Alàs and the General Council organized the creation of the [Justice Courts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Andorra#Judicial_branch "Politics of Andorra") (La Cort de Justicia) in 1433 with the co-princes and the collection of taxes like foc i lloc (literally "fire and site", a national tax active since then).[\[47\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200978,_79,_80,_81,_88,_89-53)[\[38\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009-44)

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/89/065_Absis_de_Sant_Miquel_d%27Engolasters.jpg/220px-065_Absis_de_Sant_Miquel_d%27Engolasters.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:065_Absis_de_Sant_Miquel_d%27Engolasters.jpg)

Apse fresco of [Sant Miquel d'Engolasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esgl%C3%A9sia_de_Sant_Miquel_d%27Engolasters "Església de Sant Miquel d'Engolasters") church, painted by Mestre de Santa Coloma during the 12th century[\[48\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-54)

Although there are remains of ecclesiastical works dating before the 9th century (Sant Vicenç d'Enclar or [Església de Santa Coloma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esgl%C3%A9sia_de_Santa_Coloma "Església de Santa Coloma")), Andorra developed exquisite [Romanesque Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanesque_Art_and_Architecture "Romanesque Art and Architecture") during the 9th through 14th centuries, particularly in the construction of churches, bridges, religious murals and statues of the [Virgin and Child](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_and_Child "Virgin and Child") ([Our Lady of Meritxell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Meritxell "Our Lady of Meritxell") being the most important).[\[38\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009-44) Nowadays, the [Romanesque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_regional_characteristics_of_Romanesque_churches#Romanesque_churches_in_Spain,_Portugal_and_Andorra "List of regional characteristics of Romanesque churches") buildings that form part of [Andorra's cultural heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_Heritage_of_Andorra "Cultural Heritage of Andorra") stand out in a remarkable way, with an emphasis on [Església de Sant Esteve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esgl%C3%A9sia_de_Sant_Esteve "Església de Sant Esteve"), [Sant Joan de Caselles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant_Joan_de_Caselles "Sant Joan de Caselles"), [Església de Sant Miquel d'Engolasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esgl%C3%A9sia_de_Sant_Miquel_d%27Engolasters "Església de Sant Miquel d'Engolasters"), [Sant Martí de la Cortinada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant_Mart%C3%AD_de_la_Cortinada "Sant Martí de la Cortinada") and the medieval bridges of [Margineda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pont_de_la_Margineda "Pont de la Margineda") and [Escalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pont_dels_Escalls "Pont dels Escalls") among many others.[\[49\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200948,_49-55)[\[50\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009150_a_194-56)

The Catalan Pyrenees were embryonic of the [Catalan language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language "Catalan language") at the end of the 11th century. Andorra was influenced by this language, which was adopted locally decades before it expanded to the rest of the Crown of Aragon.[\[51\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-57)

The local economy during the [Middle Ages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Ages "Middle Ages") was based on livestock, agriculture, furs and weavers. Later, at the end of the 11th century, the first [iron foundries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomery#Early_to_Medieval_Europe "Bloomery") began to appear in Northern Parishes like [Ordino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordino "Ordino"), much appreciated by the master artisans who developed the art of the forges, an important economic activity in the country from the 15th century.[\[38\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009-44)

### 16th to 18th centuries

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=7 "Edit section: 16th to 18th centuries")]

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fc/Tribunal_de_Corts_d%27Andorra.JPG/220px-Tribunal_de_Corts_d%27Andorra.JPG)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Tribunal_de_Corts_d%27Andorra.JPG)

Main hall of [Tribunal de Corts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#Law_and_criminal_justice) (High Court of Justice) inside [Casa de la Vall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casa_de_la_Vall "Casa de la Vall"), the central Judiciary Court of Andorra

In 1601 the [Tribunal de Corts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#Law_and_criminal_justice) (High Court of Justice) was created as a result of [Huguenot rebellions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huguenot_rebellions "Huguenot rebellions") in [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_modern_France "Early modern France"), [Inquisition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Inquisition "Spanish Inquisition") courts coming from [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habsburg_Spain "Habsburg Spain") and [witchcraft-related beliefs native to the area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_mythology_about_witches "Catalan mythology about witches"), in the context of the [Reformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reformation "Reformation") and [Counter-Reformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-Reformation "Counter-Reformation").[\[52\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTELlop_Rovira199844,_45,_47,_48,_50-58)[\[53\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton2009108,_109-59)[\[54\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009238,_239-60)

With the passage of time, the co-title to Andorra passed to the kings of [Navarre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Navarre "Kingdom of Navarre"). After [Henry III of Navarre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_IV_of_France "Henry IV of France") became [king of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_of_France "King of France"), he issued an edict in 1607 that established the head of the French state and the bishop of Urgell as [Co-Princes of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-Princes_of_Andorra "Co-Princes of Andorra"), a political arrangement that still holds.

During 1617, communal councils form the sometent (popular militia or army) to deal with the rise of bandolerisme ([brigandage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigandage "Brigandage")) and the Consell de la Terra was defined and structured in terms of its composition, organization and competences current today.[\[55\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTELlop_Rovira199844,_45,_47,_48,_50,_53,_54,_56-61)

Andorra continued with the same economic system that it had during the 12th–14th centuries with a large production of metallurgy (fargues, a system similar to Farga Catalana) and with the introduction of tobacco circa 1692 and import trade. In 1371 and 1448, the co-princes ratified the fair of Andorra la Vella, the most important annual national festival commercially ever since.[\[56\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTELlop_Rovira199814-62)[\[57\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTELlop_Rovira199815-63)[\[58\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton2009134-64)

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/82/Building_in_Ordino._Andorra_216.jpg/220px-Building_in_Ordino._Andorra_216.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Building_in_Ordino._Andorra_216.jpg)

[Manor house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manor_house "Manor house") of the Rossell family in [Ordino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordino "Ordino"), [Casa Rossell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casa_Rossell "Casa Rossell"), built in 1611. The family also owned the largest ironwork forges in Andorra as Farga Rossell and Farga del Serrat.[\[59\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-65)

The country had a unique and experienced guild of weavers, Confraria de Paraires i Teixidors, in [Escaldes-Engordany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escaldes-Engordany "Escaldes-Engordany"). Founded in 1604, it took advantage of the local thermal waters. By this time, the country was characterized by the social system of prohoms (wealthy society) and casalers (rest of the population with smaller economic acquisition), deriving from the tradition of [pubilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pubilla "Pubilla") and [hereu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heir "Heir").[\[60\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTELlop_Rovira199820,_21-66)[\[61\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton2009106,_107-67)[\[62\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton2009105,_106,_107,_140,_141-68)[\[63\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009263_a_270-69)

Three centuries after its foundation, the Consell de la Terra located its headquarters and the Tribunal de Corts in [Casa de la Vall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casa_de_la_Vall "Casa de la Vall") in 1702. The [manor house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manor_house "Manor house") built in 1580 served as a noble fortress of the Busquets family. Inside the parliament was placed the Closet of the six keys (Armari de les sis claus), representative of each Andorran parish, where the [Andorran constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorran_constitution "Andorran constitution") and other documents and laws were later kept.[\[64\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTELlop_Rovira199860-70)[\[65\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton200982-71)

In both the [Reapers' War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reapers%27_War "Reapers' War") and the [War of the Spanish Succession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_Spanish_Succession "War of the Spanish Succession"), the Andorran people (while professing to be a neutral country) supported the [Catalans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalans "Catalans") who saw their [rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_constitutions "Catalan constitutions") reduced in [1716](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nueva_Planta_decrees "Nueva Planta decrees"). The reaction was the promotion of Catalan writings in Andorra, with cultural works such as the *Book of Privileges* (*Llibre de Privilegis de 1674*), *Manual Digest* (1748) by Antoni Fiter i Rossell or the *Polità andorrà* (1763) by Antoni Puig.[\[66\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009229-72)[\[67\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTELlop_Rovira199849_a_52,_i_57,_58-73)

### 19th century: the New Reform and the Andorran Question

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=8 "Edit section: 19th century: the New Reform and the Andorran Question")]

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6a/Guillem_d%27Areny-Plandolit_%28cropped%29.png/170px-Guillem_d%27Areny-Plandolit_%28cropped%29.png)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Guillem_d%27Areny-Plandolit_\(cropped\).png)

[Guillem d'Areny-Plandolit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillem_d%27Areny-Plandolit "Guillem d'Areny-Plandolit") led the New Reform of 1866.

After the [French Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Revolution "French Revolution"), [Napoleon I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleon_I "Napoleon I") reestablished the Co-Principate in 1809 and removed the French medieval title. In 1812–1813, the [First French Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_French_Empire "First French Empire") annexed Catalonia during the [Peninsular War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peninsular_War "Peninsular War") (*Guerra Peninsular*) and divided the region into four [départements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D%C3%A9partement "Département"), with Andorra as a part of the district of [Puigcerdà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puigcerd%C3%A0 "Puigcerdà"). In 1814, an imperial decree reestablished the independence and economy of Andorra.[\[68\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009172-74)[\[69\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton2009172-75)[\[70\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009342%E2%80%93343-76)

During this period, Andorra's late medieval institutions and rural culture remained largely unchanged. In 1866, the [syndic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syndic "Syndic") [Guillem d'Areny-Plandolit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillem_d%27Areny-Plandolit "Guillem d'Areny-Plandolit") led the reformist group in a Council General of 24 members elected by suffrage limited to heads of families. The Council General replaced the aristocratic oligarchy that previously ruled the state.[\[71\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-ReferenceA-77)

The New Reform (*Nova Reforma*) began after ratification by both Co-Princes and established the basis of the [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Andorra "Constitution of Andorra")[\[72\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-78) and symbols – such as the [tricolour flag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Andorra "Flag of Andorra") – of Andorra. A new [service economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service_economy "Service economy") arose as a demand of the valley inhabitants and began to build infrastructure such as hotels, spa resorts, roads and telegraph lines.[\[73\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009192%E2%80%93193-79)[\[74\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton2009191%E2%80%93193-80)[\[75\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009345%E2%80%93347-81)

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/47/Revoluci%C3%B3_de_1881_d%27Andorra.jpg/190px-Revoluci%C3%B3_de_1881_d%27Andorra.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Revoluci%C3%B3_de_1881_d%27Andorra.jpg)

[Canillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canillo "Canillo") scenery during the Revolution of 1881[\[76\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-82)

The authorities of the Co-Princes banned casinos and betting houses throughout the country. The ban resulted in an economic conflict and the Revolution of 1881, which began when revolutionaries assaulted the house of the syndic on 8 December 1880, and established the Provisional Revolutionary Council led by Joan Pla i Calvo and Pere Baró i Mas. The Provisional Revolutionary Council allowed for the construction of casinos and spas by foreign companies.[\[77\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009198%E2%80%93203-83) From 7 to 9 June 1881, the loyalists of [Canillo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canillo "Canillo") and [Encamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encamp "Encamp") reconquered the parishes of [Ordino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordino "Ordino") and [La Massana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Massana "La Massana") by establishing contact with the revolutionary forces in [Escaldes-Engordany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escaldes-Engordany "Escaldes-Engordany").[\[78\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEPeruga_Guerrero199859%E2%80%9363-84) After a day of combat the [Treaty of the Bridge of Escalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pont_dels_Escalls "Pont dels Escalls") was signed on 10 June.[\[79\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMinisteri_d'Educaci%C3%B3,_Joventut_i_Esports199658%E2%80%9365-85)[\[80\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009194%E2%80%93195-86)[\[81\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009348%E2%80%93350-87) The council was replaced and new elections were held. The economic situation worsened, as the populace was divided over the *Qüestió d'Andorra* – the "Andorran Question" in relation to the [Eastern Question](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Question "Eastern Question").[\[82\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEPeruga_Guerrero199864%E2%80%9368-88) The struggles continued between pro-bishops, pro-French, and nationalists based on the troubles of Canillo in 1882 and 1885.[\[83\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMinisteri_d'Educaci%C3%B3,_Joventut_i_Esports199667%E2%80%9370-89)[\[84\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGuillamet_Anton2009198%E2%80%93203-90)[\[85\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009352%E2%80%93353-91)

Andorra participated in the cultural movement of the Catalan [Renaixença](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renaixen%C3%A7a "Renaixença"). Between 1882 and 1887, the first academic schools were formed where trilingualism coexisted with the official language, Catalan. [Romantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanticism "Romanticism") authors from [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Third_Republic "French Third Republic") and [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Restoration_\(Spain\) "Restoration (Spain)") reported the awakening of the [national consciousness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romantic_nationalism "Romantic nationalism") of the country. [Jacint Verdaguer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacint_Verdaguer "Jacint Verdaguer") lived in Ordino during the 1880s where he wrote and shared works related to the Renaixença with writer and photographer [Joaquim de Riba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casa_Rossell "Casa Rossell").

In 1848, [Fromental Halévy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fromental_Hal%C3%A9vy "Fromental Halévy") had premiered the opera *[Le val d'Andorre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_val_d%27Andorre "Le val d'Andorre")* to great success in Europe, where the national consciousness of the valleys was exposed in the romantic work during the Peninsular War.[\[86\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEPeruga_Guerrero199878%E2%80%9381-92)[\[87\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMinisteri_d'Educaci%C3%B3,_Joventut_i_Esports199674-93)[\[88\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FOOTNOTEArmengol_Aleix2009354%E2%80%93357-94)

### 20th and 21st century: Modernisation of the country and constitutional Andorra

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=9 "Edit section: 20th and 21st century: Modernisation of the country and constitutional Andorra")]

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/07/Skossyreff_Writing_Constitution.png/180px-Skossyreff_Writing_Constitution.png)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Skossyreff_Writing_Constitution.png)

[Boris Skossyreff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Skossyreff "Boris Skossyreff"), briefly self-proclaimed King of Andorra in 1934

In 1933 France occupied Andorra following social unrest which occurred before elections due to the [Revolution of 1933](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorran_Revolution "Andorran Revolution") and the FHASA strikes (Vagues de FHASA); the revolt led by [Joves Andorrans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Andorrans "Young Andorrans") (a [labour union group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trade_union "Trade union") related to the Spanish [CNT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confederaci%C3%B3n_Nacional_del_Trabajo "Confederación Nacional del Trabajo") and [FAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federaci%C3%B3n_Anarquista_Ib%C3%A9rica "Federación Anarquista Ibérica")) called for political reforms,[\[89\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-95) the [universal suffrage vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_suffrage "Universal suffrage") of all Andorrans and acted in defense of the rights of local and foreign workers during the construction of FHASA's hydroelectric power station in [Encamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encamp "Encamp").[\[90\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-96) On 5 April 1933 Joves Andorrans seized the Andorran Parliament.[\[91\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-97) These actions were preceded by the arrival of Colonel René-Jules Baulard with 50 [gendarmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Gendarmerie "National Gendarmerie") and the mobilization of 200 local militias or sometent led by the Síndic Francesc Cairat.[\[92\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-98)

On 6 July 1934, adventurer and nobleman [Boris Skossyreff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Skossyreff "Boris Skossyreff"), with his promise of freedoms and modernization of the country and wealth through the establishment of a tax haven and foreign investments, received the support of the members of the General Council to proclaim himself the sovereign of Andorra. On 8 July 1934 Boris issued a proclamation in Urgell, declaring himself Boris I, King of Andorra,[\[93\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-99) simultaneously declaring war on the Bishop of Urgell and approving the King's constitution on 10 July.[\[94\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-100) He was arrested by the Co-Prince and Bishop [Justí Guitart i Vilardebó](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Just%C3%AD_Guitart_i_Vilardeb%C3%B3 "Justí Guitart i Vilardebó") and their authorities on 20 July and ultimately expelled from [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Spanish_Republic "Second Spanish Republic").[\[95\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-101) From 1936 until 1940, a French military detachment of [Garde Mobile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garde_Mobile "Garde Mobile") led by well-known Colonel René-Jules Baulard was garrisoned in Andorra to secure the principality against disruption from the [Spanish Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Civil_War "Spanish Civil War")[\[96\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-102) and [Francoist Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francoist_Spain "Francoist Spain")[\[97\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-103) and also face the rise of [Republicanism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republicanism "Republicanism") in the aftermath of the 1933 Revolution.[\[98\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-104) During the Spanish Civil War, the inhabitants of Andorra welcomed refugees from both sides, and many of them settled permanently in the country thus contributing to the subsequent economic boom and the entry into the [capitalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitalism "Capitalism") era of Andorra.[\[99\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-105)[\[100\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-qucut.com-106) Francoist troops reached the Andorran border in the later stages of the war.[\[101\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-107)

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/99/22B_andorra_2.jpg/220px-22B_andorra_2.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:22B_andorra_2.jpg)

Enthronement as Co-Prince in 1942 of Bishop [Ramón Iglesias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramon_Iglesias_i_Navarri "Ramon Iglesias i Navarri") (centre). The local comite was led by Francesc Cairat (left), the [First General Syndic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_First_Syndics_of_the_General_Council "List of First Syndics of the General Council") with the longest regencie, from 1936 to 1960.[\[102\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-108)

During World War II, Andorra remained neutral and was an important smuggling route between [Vichy France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vichy_France "Vichy France") and Francoist Spain.[\[103\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-109) Many Andorrans criticized the passivity of the General Council for impeding both the entry and expulsion of foreigners and refugees, committing economic crimes,[\[104\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-110) reducing the rights of citizens[\[105\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-111) and sympathy with [Francoism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francoism "Francoism").[\[106\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-112)[\[107\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-iec.cat-113) General Council members justified the council's political and diplomatic actions as necessary for Andorra's survival and the protection of its sovereignty. Andorra was relatively unscathed by the two world wars and the Spanish Civil War.[\[107\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-iec.cat-113)[\[108\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-114) Certain [groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resistance_during_World_War_II "Resistance during World War II") formed to help victims of oppression in [Nazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Germany "Nazi Germany")-occupied countries, while participating in smuggling to help Andorra survive. Among the most prominent was the [Hostal Palanques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hostal_Palanques "Hostal Palanques") Evasion Network Command, which, in contact with the British [MI6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MI6 "MI6"), helped almost 400 fugitives,[\[109\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-115) among whom were [Allied](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allies_of_World_War_II "Allies of World War II") military personnel.[\[110\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-116)[\[111\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-117) The Command remained active between 1941 and 1944, although there were struggles with [pro-Axis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axis_powers "Axis powers") informers and [Gestapo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gestapo "Gestapo") agents in Andorra.[\[112\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-118)

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/03/23-24.10.67._De_Gaulle_en_Andorre_%281967%29_-_53Fi5569.jpg/220px-23-24.10.67._De_Gaulle_en_Andorre_%281967%29_-_53Fi5569.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:23-24.10.67._De_Gaulle_en_Andorre_\(1967\)_-_53Fi5569.jpg)

Co-Prince [Charles de Gaulle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_de_Gaulle "Charles de Gaulle") in the streets of Sant Julià de Lòria in Andorra, October 1967

In the capital city there was a smuggling black market of propaganda, culture and cinematic art not favorable to totalitarian regimes, promulgated in such places as the Hotel Mirador or the Casino Hotel,[\[113\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-119) as a meeting place for [Free French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_France "Free France") forces and a route for escorting crashed Allied pilots out of Europe.[\[114\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-120) The network was maintained after the war, when film societies were formed, where movies, music and books [censored in Franco's Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Censorship_in_Francoist_Spain "Censorship in Francoist Spain") were imported, becoming an anti-censorship attraction for the Catalan or foreign public even within Andorra.[\[100\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-qucut.com-106) Andorran Group (Agrupament Andorrà), an [anti-fascist organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-fascism "Anti-fascism") linked to the Occitanie's [French Resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Resistance "French Resistance"), accused the French representative (veguer) of collaboration with [Nazism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazism "Nazism").[\[115\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-121)

The Andorran opening to the [capitalist economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitalist_economy "Capitalist economy") resulted in two axes: mass tourism and the country's tax exemption. The first steps toward the capitalist boom date from the 1930s, with the construction of FHASA[\[116\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-iec.cat1-122) and the creation of professional banking[\[117\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-123) with [Banc Agrícol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andbank "Andbank") (1930) and [Crèdit Andorrà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cr%C3%A8dit_Andorr%C3%A0 "Crèdit Andorrà") (1949), later with [Banca Mora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mora_Banc_Grup "Mora Banc Grup") (1952), [Banca Cassany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banca_Privada_d%27Andorra "Banca Privada d'Andorra") (1958) and SOBANCA (1960). Shortly after, activities such as skiing and shopping become a tourist attraction, with the inauguration of ski resorts and cultural entities in the late 1930s.[\[116\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-iec.cat1-122)[\[118\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-124) All in all, a renovated hotel industry has developed. In April 1968 a social health insurance system was created ([CASS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caixa_Andorrana_de_Seguretat_Social "Caixa Andorrana de Seguretat Social")).[\[119\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-125)

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c5/Andorra_-_panoramio_%282%29.jpg/220px-Andorra_-_panoramio_%282%29.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Andorra_-_panoramio_\(2\).jpg)

Streets of the city centre of Andorra la Vella in 1986. From the same year until 1989 Andorra normalized the economic treaties with the [EEC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Economic_Community "European Economic Community").[\[120\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-126)[\[121\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-127)

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/77/Gilbert_Saboya_Sebastian_Kurz_%2813927283877%29.jpg/220px-Gilbert_Saboya_Sebastian_Kurz_%2813927283877%29.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Gilbert_Saboya_Sebastian_Kurz_\(13927283877\).jpg)

Foreign Minister of Andorra [Gilbert Saboya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilbert_Saboya_Sunye "Gilbert Saboya Sunye") meeting Austrian foreign minister [Sebastian Kurz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Kurz "Sebastian Kurz") at the [Committee of Ministers of the Council of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_of_Ministers_of_the_Council_of_Europe "Committee of Ministers of the Council of Europe") in 2014

The Andorran government necessarily involved planning, projection and forecasts for the future: with the official visit of the French co-prince [Charles de Gaulle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_de_Gaulle "Charles de Gaulle") in 1967 and 1969, it was given approval for the economic boom and national demands within the framework of [human rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_rights_in_Andorra "Human rights in Andorra") and international openness.[\[122\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-128)[\[123\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-129)

Andorra experienced an era commonly known as the "Andorran dream"[\[124\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-130) (similar to the [American Dream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Dream "American Dream")) along with the [Trente Glorieuses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trente_Glorieuses "Trente Glorieuses"): the mass culture rooted the country experiencing radical changes in the economy and culture. Proof of this was [Ràdio Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%A0dio_Andorra "Ràdio Andorra"), the top musical radio station in Europe in this period,[\[125\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-131) with guests and speakers of great importance promoting musical hits of [chanson française](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chanson_fran%C3%A7aise "Chanson française"), [swing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swing_\(dance\) "Swing (dance)"), [rhythm & blues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhythm_%26_blues "Rhythm & blues"), [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz "Jazz"), [rock and roll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_and_roll "Rock and roll") and [American country music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music "Country music").[\[126\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-132) During this period Andorra achieved a GDP per capita and a life expectancy higher than the most standard countries of the current economy.[\[116\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-iec.cat1-122)[\[127\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-133)

Given its relative isolation, Andorra has existed outside the mainstream of European history, with few ties to countries other than France, Spain and [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal "Portugal"). But in recent times its thriving tourist industry, along with developments in transport and communications, have removed the country from its isolation. Since 1976 the country has seen the need to reform Andorran institutions due to anachronisms in sovereignty, human rights and the balance of powers as well as the need to adapt legislation to modern demands. In 1982 a first separation of powers took place when instituting the Govern d'Andorra, under the name of the executive board (Consell Executiu), chaired by the first prime minister [Òscar Ribas Reig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%92scar_Ribas_Reig "Òscar Ribas Reig") with the co-princes' approval.[\[128\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-134) In 1989 the Principality signed an agreement with the [European Economic Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Economic_Community "European Economic Community") to regularize trade relations.[\[129\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-135)

Its political system was modernized in 1993 after the [Andorran constitutional referendum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorran_constitutional_referendum,_1993 "Andorran constitutional referendum, 1993"), when the [constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_Andorra "Constitution of Andorra") was drafted by the co-princes and the General Council and approved on 14 March[\[130\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-NS-136) by 74.2% of voters, with a 76% turnout.[\[131\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-137) The [first elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorran_parliamentary_election,_1993 "Andorran parliamentary election, 1993") under the new constitution were held later in the year.[\[130\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-NS-136) The same year, Andorra became a member of the United Nations and the [Council of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Europe "Council of Europe").[\[132\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-138)

Andorra formalized diplomatic relations with the United States in 1996, participating in the 51st [UN General Assembly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UN_General_Assembly "UN General Assembly"). First General Syndic [Marc Forné](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Forn%C3%A9_Moln%C3%A9 "Marc Forné Molné") took part in a speech in Catalan in the General Assembly to defend the reform of the organization, and after three days he took part in the [Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_Assembly_of_the_Council_of_Europe "Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe") to defend Andorra's linguistic rights and economy.[\[133\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-139) In 2006 a monetary agreement with the European Union was formalized that allows Andorra to use the [euro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euro "Euro") officially, as well as minting its own euro coins.[\[134\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-140)[\[135\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-141)

## Politics

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=10 "Edit section: Politics")]

Main article: [Politics of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Andorra "Politics of Andorra")

Main article: [Co-princes of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-princes_of_Andorra "Co-princes of Andorra")

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/30/Mons._Vives_%2830612833490%29.jpg/150px-Mons._Vives_%2830612833490%29.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Mons._Vives_\(30612833490\).jpg)

**[Joan Enric Vives i Sicília](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Enric_Vives_i_Sic%C3%ADlia "Joan Enric Vives i Sicília")**\
 – [Bishop of Urgell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_of_Urgell "Bishop of Urgell") since 12 May 2003

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c3/Emmanuel_Macron_%28cropped%29.jpg/132px-Emmanuel_Macron_%28cropped%29.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Emmanuel_Macron_\(cropped\).jpg)

**[Emmanuel Macron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Macron "Emmanuel Macron")**\
 – [President of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_France "President of France") since 14 May 2017

Andorra is a parliamentary [co-principality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-principality "Co-principality") with the [bishop of Urgell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_of_Urgell "Bishop of Urgell") and the president of France as [co-princes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Co-Princes_of_Andorra "List of Co-Princes of Andorra").[\[136\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:1-142) This peculiarity makes the president of France, in his capacity as [prince of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_of_Andorra "Prince of Andorra"), an elected monarch, although he is not elected by a popular vote of the Andorran people. The politics of Andorra take place in a framework of a [parliamentary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliamentary_system "Parliamentary system") [representative democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representative_democracy "Representative democracy") with a [unicameral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicameral "Unicameral") legislature, and of a [pluriform](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/pluriform "wikt:pluriform") multi-party system. The [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_heads_of_government_of_Andorra "List of heads of government of Andorra") is the [chief executive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Head_of_government "Head of government").

The current prime minister is [Xavier Espot Zamora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xavier_Espot_Zamora "Xavier Espot Zamora") of the [Democrats for Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democrats_for_Andorra "Democrats for Andorra") (DA). [Executive power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_power "Executive power") is exercised by the government. Legislative power is vested in both government and parliament.

The Parliament of Andorra is known as the [General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Council_\(Andorra\) "General Council (Andorra)"). The General Council consists of between 28 and 42 councillors.[\[136\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:1-142) The councillors serve for four-year terms, and elections are held between the 30th and 40th days following the dissolution of the previous Council.

Half are elected in equal numbers by each of the seven administrative parishes, and the other half of the councillors are elected in a single national constituency. Voters vote for parties, not candidates; a voter votes for a party for the parish councillors and a party for the at-large councilors, and the winners come from party lists. Fifteen days after the election, the councillors hold their inauguration. During this session, the [General Syndic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_general_syndics_of_the_General_Council "List of general syndics of the General Council"), who is the head of the General Council, and the Subsyndic General, his assistant, are elected. Eight days later, the Council convenes once more. During this session, the prime minister is chosen from among the councillors.

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8e/Casa_de_la_Vall_-_18.jpg/220px-Casa_de_la_Vall_-_18.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Casa_de_la_Vall_-_18.jpg)

Casa de la Vall, the historical and ceremonial Andorran Parliament

Candidates can be proposed by a minimum of one-fifth of the councillors. The Council then elects the candidate with the absolute majority of votes to be prime minister. The Syndic General then notifies the co-princes, who in turn appoint the elected candidate as the prime minister of Andorra. The General Council is also responsible for proposing and passing laws. Bills may be presented to the council as Private Members' Bills by three of the local Parish Councils jointly or by at least one tenth of the citizens of Andorra.

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/49/Casa_de_la_Vall%2C_Andorra_la_Vieja%2C_Andorra%2C_2013-12-30%2C_DD_03.JPG/220px-Casa_de_la_Vall%2C_Andorra_la_Vieja%2C_Andorra%2C_2013-12-30%2C_DD_03.JPG)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Casa_de_la_Vall,_Andorra_la_Vieja,_Andorra,_2013-12-30,_DD_03.JPG)

The [New Parliament of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Parliament_of_Andorra "New Parliament of Andorra"), headquarters of the [General Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Council_of_Andorra "General Council of Andorra") since 2011, next to [Casa de la Vall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casa_de_la_Vall "Casa de la Vall")

The council also approves the annual budget of the principality. The government must submit the proposed budget for parliamentary approval at least two months before the previous budget expires. If the budget is not approved by the first day of the next year, the previous budget is extended until a new one is approved. Once any bill is approved, the Syndic General is responsible for presenting it to the co-princes so that they may sign and enact it.

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7b/Els_7_poetes%2C_Casa_de_la_Vall%2C_Consell_General.JPG/220px-Els_7_poetes%2C_Casa_de_la_Vall%2C_Consell_General.JPG)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Els_7_poetes,_Casa_de_la_Vall,_Consell_General.JPG)

The centre of government in [Andorra la Vella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_la_Vella "Andorra la Vella")

If the prime minister is not satisfied with the council, he may request that the co-princes dissolve the council and order new elections. In turn, the councillors have the power to remove the prime minister from office. After a motion of censure is approved by at least one-fifth of the councillors, the council will vote and if it receives the absolute majority of votes, the prime minister is removed.\[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*]

## Law and criminal justice

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=11 "Edit section: Law and criminal justice")]

Main article: [Law of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_Andorra "Law of Andorra")

The judiciary is composed of the Magistrates Court, the Criminal Law Court, the High Court of Andorra, and the Constitutional Court. The High Court of Justice is composed of five judges: one appointed by the prime minister, one each by the co-princes, one by the Syndic General, and one by the judges and magistrates. It is presided over by the member appointed by the Syndic General and the judges hold office for six-year terms.[\[137\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-143)

The magistrates and judges are appointed by the High Court, as is the president of the Criminal Law Court. The High Court also appoints members of the Office of the Attorney General. The Constitutional Court is responsible for interpreting the Constitution and reviewing all appeals of unconstitutionality against laws and treaties. It is composed of four judges, one appointed by each of the co-princes and two by the General Council. They serve eight-year terms. The Court is presided over by one of the judges on a two-year rotation so that each judge at one point will preside over the Court.

## Foreign relations, defence and security

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=12 "Edit section: Foreign relations, defence and security")]

Main article: [Foreign relations of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreign_relations_of_Andorra "Foreign relations of Andorra")

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/39/Bruxelles_rue_de_la_Montagne_10.jpg/220px-Bruxelles_rue_de_la_Montagne_10.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Bruxelles_rue_de_la_Montagne_10.jpg)

The embassy of Andorra in [Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels "Brussels")

Andorra does not have its own armed forces,[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-cia-3) although there is a small ceremonial army. Responsibility for defending the nation rests primarily with France and Spain.[\[138\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-144) However, in case of emergencies or natural disasters, the [Sometent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sometent "Sometent") (an alarm) is called and all able-bodied men between 21 and 60 of Andorran nationality must serve.[\[139\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-145)[\[140\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-recarrega.net-146) This is why all Andorrans, and especially the head of each house (usually the eldest able-bodied man of a house) should, by law, keep a rifle, even though the law also states that the police will offer a firearm in case of need.[\[140\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-recarrega.net-146) Andorra is a full member of the United Nations (UN), the [Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization_for_Security_and_Co-operation_in_Europe "Organization for Security and Co-operation in Europe") (OSCE), and has a special agreement with the European Union (EU). After the constitution was ratified, Andorra would later establish diplomatic relations with their major allies aside from neighbours France and Spain such as the United Kingdom in 1994 and the United States in 1995. It also has observer status at the [World Trade Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Trade_Organization "World Trade Organization") (WTO).[\[141\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-147) On 16 October 2020, Andorra became the 190th member of the [International Monetary Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Monetary_Fund "International Monetary Fund") (IMF), during the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic "COVID-19 pandemic").[\[142\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-148)[\[143\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-149)

### Military

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=13 "Edit section: Military")]

Andorra has a small army, which has historically been raised or reconstituted at various dates, but has never in modern times amounted to a standing army. The basic principle of Andorran defence is that all able-bodied men are available to fight if called upon by the summoning of the [Sometent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sometent "Sometent") (a civil defense organization of Andorra, made up of the heads of households). The Sometent was used sparingly against French "Lutheran" raids during the late 16th century. Being a [landlocked country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landlocked_country "Landlocked country"), Andorra has no navy.

Before [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I "World War I"), Andorra maintained an armed [militia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Militia "Militia") force of about 600 part-time militiamen under the supervision of a Captain (Capità or Cap de Sometent) and a Lieutenant (Desener or Lloctinent del Capità). This body was not liable for service outside the principality and was commanded by two officials (veguers) appointed by France and the Bishop of Urgell.[\[144\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-150)

In the modern era, the army has consisted of a very small body of volunteers willing to undertake [ceremonial duties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honor_guard "Honor guard"). Uniforms and weaponry were handed down from generation to generation within families and communities.[\[145\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-elperiodic.ad-151)

The army's role in internal security was largely taken over by the formation of the [Police Corps of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_Corps_of_Andorra "Police Corps of Andorra") in 1931. Brief civil disorder associated with the elections of 1933 led to assistance being sought from the French [National Gendarmerie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Gendarmerie "National Gendarmerie"),[\[146\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-152) with a detachment resident in Andorra for two months under the command of René-Jules Baulard.\[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] The Andorran Police was reformed in the following year, with eleven soldiers appointed to supervisory roles.[\[147\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-153) The force consisted of six [corporals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporal "Corporal"), one for each parish (although there are currently seven parishes, there were only six until 1978), plus four junior staff officers to co-ordinate action, and a commander with the rank of major. It was the responsibility of the six corporals, each in his own parish, to be able to raise a fighting force from among the able-bodied men of the parish.

The only permanent section of the present-day Sometent is a twelve-man ceremonial unit. However, all able-bodied men are technically [available for military service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conscription "Conscription"),[\[148\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-154) with a requirement for each family to have access to a firearm. An [area weapon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shot_\(pellet\) "Shot (pellet)"), such as a shotgun per household, is unregulated; however, [ranged weapons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bullet "Bullet"), such as pistols and rifles, require a license.[\[145\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-elperiodic.ad-151) The army has not fought for more than 700 years, and its main responsibility is to present the [flag of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Andorra "Flag of Andorra") at official ceremonial functions.[\[149\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-155)[\[150\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-156) According to [Marc Forné Molné](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Forn%C3%A9_Moln%C3%A9 "Marc Forné Molné"), Andorra's military budget is strictly from voluntary donations, and the availability of full-time volunteers.[\[151\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-157)

The only more recent general emergency call to the Sometent was given during the floods of 1982 in the Catalan Pyrenees (when 12 Andorran citizens perished),[\[152\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-158) to help the population and establish a public order along with the local police units.[\[153\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-159)

### Police Corps

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=14 "Edit section: Police Corps")]

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9d/Skoda_Policia_-_Andorra_%282022%29.jpg/220px-Skoda_Policia_-_Andorra_%282022%29.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Skoda_Policia_-_Andorra_\(2022\).jpg)

[National Police](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_Corps_of_Andorra "Police Corps of Andorra") patrolling the central area of the capital city

Main article: [Police Corps of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_Corps_of_Andorra "Police Corps of Andorra")

Andorra maintains a small but modern and well-equipped internal police force, with around 240 police officers supported by civilian assistants. The principal services supplied by the corps are uniformed community policing, criminal detection, border control, and traffic policing. There are also small specialist units including police dogs, mountain rescue, and a bomb disposal team.[\[154\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-160)

#### GIPA

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=15 "Edit section: GIPA")]

The *Grup d'Intervenció Policia d'Andorra*' (GIPA) is a small [special forces squad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_tactical_unit "Police tactical unit") trained in [counter-terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Counter-terrorism "Counter-terrorism"), and [hostage recovery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hostage_negotiator "Hostage negotiator") tasks. Although it is the [closest in style to an active military force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramilitary "Paramilitary"), it is part of the [Police Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_Corps_of_Andorra "Police Corps of Andorra"), and not the army. As terrorist and hostage situations are a rare threat to the country, the GIPA is commonly assigned to prisoner escort duties, and at other times to routine policing.[\[155\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-161)

### Fire brigade

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=16 "Edit section: Fire brigade")]

The Andorran Fire Brigade, with headquarters at [Santa Coloma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Coloma_d%27Andorra "Santa Coloma d'Andorra"), operates from four modern fire stations, and has a staff of around 120 firefighters. The service is equipped with 16 heavy appliances (fire tenders, turntable ladders, and specialist four-wheel drive vehicles), four light support vehicles (cars and vans) and four ambulances.[\[156\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-162)

Historically, the families of the six ancient parishes of Andorra maintained local arrangements to assist each other in fighting fires. The first fire pump purchased by the government was acquired in 1943. Serious fires which lasted for two days in December 1959 led to calls for a permanent fire service, and the Andorran Fire Brigade was formed on 21 April 1961.[\[157\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-163)

The fire service maintains full-time cover with five fire crews on duty at any time: two at the brigade's headquarters in Santa Coloma, and one crew at each of the other three fire stations.[\[158\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-164)

### Physical geography

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=19 "Edit section: Physical geography")]

Due to its location in the eastern [Pyrenees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrenees "Pyrenees") mountain range, Andorra consists predominantly of rugged mountains, the highest being the [Coma Pedrosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coma_Pedrosa "Coma Pedrosa") at 2,946 metres (9,665 ft), and the average elevation of Andorra is 1,996 metres (6,549 ft).[\[159\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-165) These are dissected by three narrow valleys in a Y shape that combine into one as the main stream, the [Gran Valira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gran_Valira "Gran Valira") river, leaves the country for Spain (at Andorra's lowest point of 840 m or 2,756 ft). Andorra's land area is 468 km2 (181 sq mi).

### Environment

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=20 "Edit section: Environment")]

[Phytogeographically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phytogeography "Phytogeography"), Andorra belongs to the Atlantic European province of the [Circumboreal Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circumboreal_Region "Circumboreal Region") within the [Boreal Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boreal_Kingdom "Boreal Kingdom"). According to the [WWF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Wide_Fund_for_Nature "World Wide Fund for Nature"), the territory of Andorra belongs to the [ecoregion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecoregion "Ecoregion") of [Pyrenees conifer and mixed forests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrenees_conifer_and_mixed_forests "Pyrenees conifer and mixed forests").[\[160\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-DinersteinOlson2017-166) Andorra had a 2018 [Forest Landscape Integrity Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_Landscape_Integrity_Index "Forest Landscape Integrity Index") mean score of 4.45/10, ranking it 127th globally out of 172 countries.[\[161\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-FLII-Supplementary-167) In Andorra [forest cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_cover "Forest cover") is around 34% of the total land area, equivalent to 16,000 hectares (ha) of forest in 2020, which was unchanged from 1990. In 2020, naturally regenerating forest covered 16,000 hectares (ha) and planted forest covered 0 hectares (ha). Of the naturally regenerating forest 0% was reported to be [primary forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_forest "Primary forest") (consisting of native tree species with no clearly visible indications of human activity) and around 0% of the forest area was found within protected areas.[\[162\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-168)[\[163\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-169)

#### Important Bird Area

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=21 "Edit section: Important Bird Area")]

The whole country has been recognised as a single [Important Bird Area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Important_Bird_Area "Important Bird Area") (IBA) by [BirdLife International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BirdLife_International "BirdLife International"), because it is important for forest and mountain birds and supports populations of [red-billed choughs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red-billed_chough "Red-billed chough"), [citril finches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citril_finch "Citril finch") and [rock buntings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_bunting "Rock bunting").[\[164\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-bli-170)

### Climate

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=22 "Edit section: Climate")]

Andorra has [alpine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpine_climate "Alpine climate"), [continental](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continental_climate "Continental climate") and [oceanic climates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oceanic_climate "Oceanic climate"), depending on altitude. Its higher elevation means there is, on average, more snow in winter and it is slightly cooler in summer. The diversity of landmarks, the different orientation of the valleys and the irregularity relief typical of the [Mediterranean climates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mediterranean_climate "Mediterranean climate") make the country have a great diversity of [microclimates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microclimate "Microclimate") that hinder the general dominance of the [high mountain climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_climate "Mountain climate"). The great differences of altitude in the minimum and maximum points, together with the influence of a Mediterranean climate, develop the climate of the Andorran Pyrenees.

When in precipitation, a global model characterized by convective and abundant rains can be defined during spring and summer, which can last until autumn (May, June and August are usually the rainiest months). In winter, however, it is less rainy, except in the highlands, subject to the influence of fronts from the [Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Europe "Atlantic Europe"), which explains the great amount of snowfall in the Andorran mountains. The temperature regime is characterized, broadly, by a temperate summer and a long and cold winter, in accordance with the mountainous condition of the Principality.[\[165\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-171)

As a small mountainous country, Andorra is highly vulnerable to [climate change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change "Climate change"). Temperatures in its high-altitude regions have risen by about 0.17°C per decade, while annual rainfall has decreased by 49 mm. These shifts are impacting water resources and snow cover, which are key elements for Andorra's tourism-driven economy.[\[168\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-174) The number of days with enough snow for [skiing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skiing "Skiing") is declining, and the [snow line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snow_line "Snow line") is retreating to higher altitudes.[\[169\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:3-175)

Although it's national [greenhouse gas emissions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenhouse_gas_emissions "Greenhouse gas emissions") are one of the lowest in the world (534 thousand tonnes emitted in 2023),[\[170\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:6-176) Andorra has a strong climate change mitigation strategy, with a focus on [renewable energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renewable_energy "Renewable energy") and energy efficiency. In its [Nationally Determined Contribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nationally_determined_contribution "Nationally determined contribution"), Andorra has committed to reducing its emissions by 55% by 2030, and to [carbon neutrality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_neutrality "Carbon neutrality") by 2050.[\[171\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-177) However, the adaptation part of the strategy is still in early stages and may be difficult and costly to implement. Given the country's reliance on tourism, speeding up adaptation is essential for building a more resilient economy.[\[172\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:32-178)

## Economy

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=23 "Edit section: Economy")]

Main article: [Economy of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economy_of_Andorra "Economy of Andorra")

Tourism, the mainstay of Andorra's economy, accounts for roughly 80% of GDP. An estimated 8 million tourists visit annually,[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-es2-27) attracted by Andorra's [duty-free](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duty-free_shopping "Duty-free shopping") status and by its summer and winter resorts.

One of the main sources of income in Andorra is from ski resorts, which total over 175 km (109 mi) of ski grounds. The sport brings in over 7 million visitors annually and an estimated 340 million euros per year, sustaining 2,000 direct and 10,000 indirect jobs at present since 2007.[\[173\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-179)

The banking sector, with its [tax haven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tax_haven "Tax haven") status, also contributes substantially to the economy with revenues raised exclusively through import tariffs (the financial and insurance sector accounts for approximately 19% of GDP[\[174\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-fs2013-180)). However, during the [European sovereign-debt crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_sovereign-debt_crisis "European sovereign-debt crisis") of the 21st century, the tourist industry suffered a decline, partly caused by a drop in the prices of goods in Spain, undercutting [duty-free shopping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duty-free_shop "Duty-free shop") and increasing unemployment. On 1 January 2012, a business tax of 10% was introduced,[\[175\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-181) followed by a sales tax of 2% a year later, which raised just over 14 million euros in its first quarter.[\[176\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-182)

Agricultural production is limited; only 1.7%[\[177\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-183) of the land is arable, and most food has to be imported. Some tobacco is grown locally. The principal livestock activity is domestic sheep raising. Manufacturing output consists mainly of cigarettes, cigars, and furniture. Andorra's natural resources include hydroelectric power, mineral water, timber, iron ore, and lead.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-cia-3)

Andorra is not a member of the European Union, but enjoys a [special relationship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra%E2%80%93European_Union_relations "Andorra–European Union relations") with it, such as being treated as an EU member for trade in manufactured goods (no tariffs) and as a non-EU member for agricultural products. Andorra lacked a currency of its own and used both the [French franc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_franc "French franc") and the [Spanish peseta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_peseta "Spanish peseta") in banking transactions until 31 December 1999, when both currencies were replaced by the EU's single currency, the euro. Coins and notes of both the franc and the peseta remained legal tender in Andorra until 31 December 2002. Andorra negotiated to issue its own euro coins, beginning in 2014.

Andorra has historically had one of the world's lowest unemployment rates. In 2023 it stood at 1.5%.[\[178\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-184)

On 31 May 2013, it was announced that Andorra intended to legislate for the introduction of an income tax by the end of June, against a background of increasing dissatisfaction with the existence of tax havens among EU members.[\[179\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-185) The announcement was made following a meeting in Paris between the Prime Minister [Antoni Martí](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoni_Mart%C3%AD "Antoni Martí") and the French President and Prince of Andorra [François Hollande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7ois_Hollande "François Hollande"). Hollande welcomed the move as part of a process of Andorra "bringing its taxation in line with international standards".[\[180\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-186)

By the mid-2010s, the financial system comprised five banking groups,[\[181\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-187) one specialised credit entity, eight investment undertaking management entities, three asset management companies, and 29 insurance companies, 14 of which are branches of foreign insurance companies authorised to operate in the principality.[\[174\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-fs2013-180) The last mergers between banks took place in 2022, bringing the Andorran financial sector to currently have 3 active banking groups.[\[182\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-188)

The population of Andorra is estimated at 79,034 (2021).[\[15\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-UN_WPP-21)[\[16\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-UN_WPP_2022-22) The [Andorrans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorrans "Andorrans") are a [Romance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romance-speaking_world "Romance-speaking world") [ethnic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_group "Ethnic group") of originally [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalans "Catalans") descent.[\[17\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-Minahan-23) The population has grown from 5,000 in 1900.

Two-thirds of residents lack Andorran nationality and do not have the right to vote in communal elections. Moreover, they are not allowed to be elected as prime minister[\[184\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-190) or to own more than 33% of the capital stock of a privately held company.[\[185\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-191)[\[186\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-192)[\[187\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-193)[\[188\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-194)

The largest national groups in Andorra are Spanish (34.3%), Andorrans (32.1%), Portuguese (10%), and French (5.6%). The remaining 18% of the population includes British, Dutch, Germans, Italians and other Europeans, as well as Argentinians, Chileans, Indians, Moroccans and Uruguayans.[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-es2-27)

The historic and [official language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_language "Official language") is [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language "Catalan language"), a [Romance language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romance_language "Romance language"). The Andorran government encourages the use of Catalan. It funds a Commission for Catalan [Toponymy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toponymy "Toponymy") in Andorra (Catalan: *Comissió de Toponímia d'Andorra*), and provides free Catalan classes to assist immigrants. Andorran television and radio stations use Catalan.

Because of immigration, historical links, and close geographic proximity, Spanish, Portuguese and French are commonly spoken. Most Andorran residents can speak one or more of these, in addition to Catalan. English is less commonly spoken among the general population, though it is understood to varying degrees in the major tourist resorts.\[*[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")*] Andorra is one of only four European countries (together with France, [Monaco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monaco "Monaco"), and [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey "Turkey"))[\[190\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-196) that have never signed the [Council of Europe Framework Convention on National Minorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framework_Convention_for_the_Protection_of_National_Minorities "Framework Convention for the Protection of National Minorities").[\[191\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-197)

## Education

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=29 "Edit section: Education")]

The Qualified Education Law (*Ilei qualificada d'educacio*) of Andorra was passed in 1993. It guarantees free, public education from age four until the end of compulsory schooling.[\[198\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:2-204)

### Schools

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=30 "Edit section: Schools")]

Children between the ages of 6 and 16 are required by law to have full-time education.[\[198\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:2-204) Education up to secondary level is provided free of charge by the government.

There are three systems of school, Andorran, French and Spanish, which use Catalan, French and Spanish languages respectively, as the main language of instruction. Parents may choose which system their children attend. All schools are built and maintained by Andorran authorities, but teachers in the French and Spanish schools are paid for the most part by France and Spain. 39% of Andorran children attend Andorran schools, 33% attend French schools, and 28% Spanish schools.

### University of Andorra

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=31 "Edit section: University of Andorra")]

The [Universitat d'Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universitat_d%27Andorra "Universitat d'Andorra") (UdA) is the state public university and is the only university in Andorra. It was established in 1997. The university provides first-level degrees in nursing, computer science, business administration, and educational sciences, in addition to higher professional education courses. The only two graduate schools in Andorra are the Nursing School and the School of Computer Science, the latter having a PhD programme.

#### Virtual Studies Centre

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=32 "Edit section: Virtual Studies Centre")]

The geographical complexity of the country as well as the small number of students prevents the University of Andorra from developing a full academic programme, and it serves principally as a centre for virtual studies, connected to Spanish and French universities. The Virtual Studies Centre (Centre d'Estudis Virtuals) at the university runs approximately 20 different academic degrees at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels in fields including tourism, law, Catalan [philology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philology "Philology"), humanities, psychology, political sciences, audiovisual communication, telecommunications engineering, and East Asia studies. The centre also runs various postgraduate programmes and continuing-education courses for professionals.

## Transport

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=33 "Edit section: Transport")]

Main article: [Transport in Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transport_in_Andorra "Transport in Andorra")

See also: [Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra%E2%80%93La_Seu_d%27Urgell_Airport "Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell Airport")

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e1/Pirineus_-_la_Seu_d%27Urgell_airport.jpg/220px-Pirineus_-_la_Seu_d%27Urgell_airport.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Pirineus_-_la_Seu_d%27Urgell_airport.jpg)

[Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra%E2%80%93La_Seu_d%27Urgell_Airport "Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell Airport"), located 12 kilometres (7 mi) away from Andorra, in [Montferrer i Castellbò](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montferrer_i_Castellb%C3%B2 "Montferrer i Castellbò") ([Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalonia "Catalonia"), Eastern Spain)

Until the 20th century, Andorra had very limited transport links to the outside world, and development of the country was affected by its physical isolation. Even now, the nearest major airports at Toulouse and Barcelona are both three hours' drive from Andorra.

Andorra has a road network of 279 km (173 mi), of which 76 km (47 mi) is unpaved. The two main roads out of [Andorra la Vella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_la_Vella "Andorra la Vella") are the CG-1 to the [Spanish border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra%E2%80%93Spain_border "Andorra–Spain border") near [Sant Julià de Lòria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant_Juli%C3%A0_de_L%C3%B2ria "Sant Julià de Lòria"), and the CG-2 to the [French border](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra%E2%80%93France_border "Andorra–France border") via the Envalira Tunnel near [El Pas de la Casa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Pas_de_la_Casa "El Pas de la Casa").[\[199\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-mobi-205) Bus services cover all metropolitan areas and many rural communities, with services on most major routes running half-hourly or more frequently during peak travel times. There are frequent long-distance bus services from Andorra to [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona "Barcelona") and [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse "Toulouse"), plus a daily tour from the former city. Bus services mostly are run by private companies, but some local ones are operated by the government.

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/70/AGC_Z_27500.jpg/220px-AGC_Z_27500.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:AGC_Z_27500.jpg)

A train at [Latour-de-Carol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latour-de-Carol "Latour-de-Carol") (La Tor de Querol), one of the two stations serving Andorra. Andorra has no railways, although the line connecting Latour-de-Carol and [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse "Toulouse"), which in turn connects to France's [TGVs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TGV "TGV") at Toulouse, runs within two kilometres (1.2 miles) of the Andorran border.

There are no airports for fixed-wing aircraft within Andorra's borders but there are, however, heliports in [La Massana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Massana "La Massana") (Camí Heliport), [Arinsal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arinsal "Arinsal") and [Escaldes-Engordany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escaldes-Engordany "Escaldes-Engordany") with commercial helicopter services[\[200\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-heliand-206)[\[201\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-helitrans-207) and an airport located in the neighbouring Spanish comarca of [Alt Urgell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alt_Urgell "Alt Urgell"), 12 kilometres (7.5 miles) south of the Andorran-Spanish border.[\[202\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-208) Since July 2015, [Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra%E2%80%93La_Seu_d%27Urgell_Airport "Andorra–La Seu d'Urgell Airport") has operated commercial flights to [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolfo_Su%C3%A1rez_Madrid%E2%80%93Barajas_Airport "Adolfo Suárez Madrid–Barajas Airport") and [Palma de Mallorca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palma_de_Mallorca_Airport "Palma de Mallorca Airport"), and is the [main hub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airline_hub "Airline hub") for [Andorra Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_Airlines "Andorra Airlines").

Nearby airports located in Spain and France provide access to international flights for the principality. The nearest airports are at [Perpignan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perpignan%E2%80%93Rivesaltes_Airport "Perpignan–Rivesaltes Airport"), France (156 kilometres or 97 miles from Andorra) and [Lleida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lleida-Alguaire_Airport "Lleida-Alguaire Airport"), Spain (160 kilometres or 99 miles from Andorra). The largest nearby airports are at Toulouse, France (165 kilometres or 103 miles from Andorra) and Barcelona, Spain (215 kilometres or 134 miles from Andorra). There are hourly bus services from both Barcelona and Toulouse airports to Andorra.

The nearest railway station is [Andorre-L'Hospitalet station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorre-L%27Hospitalet_station "Andorre-L'Hospitalet station") 10 km (6 mi) east of Andorra which is on the 1,435 mm (4 ft 8+1⁄2 in)[-gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_gauge "Standard gauge") line from [Latour-de-Carol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latour-de-Carol "Latour-de-Carol") (25 km or 16 mi) southeast of Andorra, to [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse "Toulouse") and on to Paris by the French [high-speed trains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TGV "TGV"). This line is operated by the [SNCF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNCF "SNCF"). Latour-de-Carol has a scenic 1,000 mm (3 ft 3+3⁄8 in) [metre gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metre_gauge "Metre gauge") [trainline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yellow_Train "Yellow Train") to [Villefranche-de-Conflent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villefranche-de-Conflent "Villefranche-de-Conflent"), as well as the SNCF's 1,435 mm gauge line connecting to [Perpignan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perpignan "Perpignan"), and the [Renfe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renfe "Renfe")'s 1,668 mm (5 ft 5+21⁄32 in) [-gauge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iberian_gauge "Iberian gauge") line to Barcelona.[\[203\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-209)[\[204\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-210) There are also direct [Intercités de Nuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intercit%C3%A9s#Night_trains "Intercités") trains between L'Hospitalet-près-l'Andorre and Paris on certain dates.[\[205\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-211)

## Media and telecommunications

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=34 "Edit section: Media and telecommunications")]

Main article: [Telecommunications in Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommunications_in_Andorra "Telecommunications in Andorra")

See also: [Andorra Telecom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_Telecom "Andorra Telecom")

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/65/RTVA_logotip.png/220px-RTVA_logotip.png)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:RTVA_logotip.png)

[RTVA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_i_Televisio_d%27Andorra "Radio i Televisio d'Andorra"), the public service television and radio broadcaster in Andorra

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9c/Andorra_Telecom_2019_logo.svg/220px-Andorra_Telecom_2019_logo.svg.png)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Andorra_Telecom_2019_logo.svg)

[Andorra Telecom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_Telecom "Andorra Telecom"), the national telecom company in Andorra

In Andorra, mobile and fixed telephone and internet services are operated exclusively by the Andorran national telecommunications company, SOM, also known as [Andorra Telecom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_Telecom "Andorra Telecom") (STA). The same company also manages the technical infrastructure for national broadcasting of digital television and radio.[\[206\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-212) In 2010 Andorra became the first country to provide a direct [optical fiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optical_fiber "Optical fiber") link to all homes ([FTTH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FTTx "FTTx")) and businesses.[\[207\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-213)

The first commercial radio station to broadcast was [Radio Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Andorra "Radio Andorra"), which was active from 1939 to 1981.[\[208\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-214)[\[209\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-215)[\[210\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-216) On 12 October 1989, the General Council established radio and television as essential public services creating and managing the entity ORTA, becoming on 13 April 2000, in the public company [Ràdio i Televisió d'Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R%C3%A0dio_i_Televisi%C3%B3_d%27Andorra "Ràdio i Televisió d'Andorra") (RTVA).[\[211\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-217) In 1990, the public radio was founded on the Radio Nacional d'Andorra. As an autochthonous television channel, there is only the national public television network Andorra Televisió, created in 1995.[\[212\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-218) Additional TV and radio stations from Spain and France are available via digital terrestrial television and IPTV.[\[213\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-219)

There are three national newspapers, *[Diari d'Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diari_d%27Andorra "Diari d'Andorra")*, *[El Periòdic d'Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Peri%C3%B2dic_d%27Andorra "El Periòdic d'Andorra")*, and *[Bondia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bondia_\(newspaper\) "Bondia (newspaper)")* as well as several local newspapers.[\[214\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-220) The history of the Andorran press begins in the period between 1917 and 1937 with the appearance of several periodicals papers such as *Les Valls d'Andorra* (1917), *Nova Andorra* (1932) and *Andorra Agrícola* (1933).[\[215\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-221) In 1974, the *Poble Andorrà* became the first regular newspaper in Andorra.[\[216\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-222) There is also an [amateur radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amateur_radio "Amateur radio") society[\[217\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-223) and news agency ANA with independent management.[\[218\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-224)

## Culture

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=35 "Edit section: Culture")]

Main article: [Culture of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_Andorra "Culture of Andorra")

See also: [Music of Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_Andorra "Music of Andorra")

Andorra is home to folk dances like the [contrapàs](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Contrap%C3%A0s\&action=edit\&redlink=1 "Contrapàs (page does not exist)") and [marratxa](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Marratxa\&action=edit\&redlink=1 "Marratxa (page does not exist)"), which survive in [Sant Julià de Lòria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant_Juli%C3%A0_de_L%C3%B2ria "Sant Julià de Lòria") especially. Andorran folk music has similarities to the music of its neighbours, but is especially [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_Catalonia "Music of Catalonia") in character, especially in the presence of dances such as the [sardana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sardana "Sardana"). Other Andorran folk dances include contrapàs in [Andorra la Vella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_la_Vella "Andorra la Vella") and Saint Anne's dance in Escaldes-Engordany. Andorra's national holiday is [Our Lady of Meritxell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Meritxell "Our Lady of Meritxell") Day, 8 September.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-cia-3)

Among the more important festivals and traditions are the Canólich Gathering in May, the Roser d'Ordino in July, the Meritxell Day (National Day of Andorra), the Andorra la Vella Fair, the [Sant Jordi Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant_Jordi "Sant Jordi"), the Santa Llúcia Fair, the Festivity from La Candelera to Canillo, the [Carnival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnival "Carnival") of Encamp, the sung of caramelles, the Festivity of [Sant Esteve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Stephen "Saint Stephen") and the Festa del Poble.[\[219\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-225)

Andorra participated regularly in the [Eurovision Song Contest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovision_Song_Contest "Eurovision Song Contest") between 2004 and 2009, being the only [participating country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_in_the_Eurovision_Song_Contest "List of countries in the Eurovision Song Contest") presenting songs in [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language "Catalan language").

In popular folklore, the best-known Andorran legends are the legend of Charlemagne, according to which this Frankish King would have founded the country, the White Lady of [Auvinyà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auviny%C3%A0 "Auvinyà"), the [Buner d'Ordino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_buner_d%27Ordino "El buner d'Ordino"), the legend of [Engolasters Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engolasters "Engolasters") and the legend of [Our Lady of Meritxell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Meritxell "Our Lady of Meritxell").

Andorran gastronomy is mainly [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_cuisine "Catalan cuisine"), although it has also adopted other elements of [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_cuisine "French cuisine") and [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_cuisine "Italian cuisine") cuisines. The cuisine of the country has similar characteristics with the neighbours of [Cerdanya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cerdanya "Cerdanya") and [Alt Urgell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alt_Urgell "Alt Urgell"), with whom it has strong cultural ties. Andorra's cuisine is marked by its nature as mountain valleys. Typical dishes of the country are [quince](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quince "Quince") [all-i-oli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aioli "Aioli"), duck with winter pear, roast lamb with nuts, pork civet, massegada cake, escarole with pears, duck confit and mushrooms, [escudella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escudella "Escudella"), spinach with raisins and pine nuts, jelly marmalade, stuffed murgues (mushrooms) with pork, [dandelion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dandelion "Dandelion") salad, and Andorran river [trout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trout "Trout"). To drink, [mulled wine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulled_wine "Mulled wine") and beer are also popular.[\[220\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-226) Some of the dishes are very common in the [mountainous regions of Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alt_Pirineu_i_Aran "Alt Pirineu i Aran"), such as [trinxat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinxat "Trinxat"), [embotits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embutido "Embutido"), cooked snails, rice with mushrooms, mountain rice and [mató](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mat%C3%B3 "Mató").[\[221\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-227)

[Pre-Romanesque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-Romanesque "Pre-Romanesque") and [Romanesque art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanesque_art "Romanesque art") are one of the most important artistic manifestations and characteristics of the Principality. The Romanesque one allows to know the formation of the [parochial communities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parishes_of_Andorra "Parishes of Andorra"), the relations of (social and political) power and the national culture. There are a total of forty Romanesque churches that stand out as being small austere ornamentation constructions, as well as [bridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanesque_architecture "Romanesque architecture"), fortresses and [manor houses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manor_houses "Manor houses") of the same period.[\[222\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-228)[\[223\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-229)

[Summer solstice fire festivals](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Summer_solstice_fire_festivals\&action=edit\&redlink=1 "Summer solstice fire festivals (page does not exist)") in the Pyrenees was included as [UNESCO Intangible cultural heritage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intangible_cultural_heritage "Intangible cultural heritage") in 2015.[\[224\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-230) Also the [Madriu-Perafita-Claror Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madriu-Perafita-Claror_Valley "Madriu-Perafita-Claror Valley") became Andorra's first, and to date its only, [UNESCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO "UNESCO") [World Heritage Site](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Heritage_Site "World Heritage Site") in 2004, with a small extension in 2006.[\[225\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-231)[\[226\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-232)

### Sports

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=36 "Edit section: Sports")]

Andorra is famous for its [winter sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_sports "Winter sports"). Andorra has the largest territory of ski slopes in the [Pyrenees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrenees "Pyrenees") (3100 hectares and about 350 km of slopes) and two ski resorts. Grandvalira is the largest and most popular resort. Other popular sports played in Andorra include [football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football "Association football"), [rugby union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby_union "Rugby union"), basketball, and [roller hockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roller_hockey_\(quad\) "Roller hockey (quad)").

For [roller hockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roller_hockey_\(quad\) "Roller hockey (quad)"), [Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_national_roller_hockey_team "Andorra national roller hockey team") usually plays in the [CERH Euro Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CERH_European_Roller_Hockey_Championship "CERH European Roller Hockey Championship") and [FIRS Roller Hockey World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIRS_Roller_Hockey_World_Cup "FIRS Roller Hockey World Cup"). In 2011, Andorra was the host country to the [2011 European League Final Eight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_European_League_Final_Eight "2011 European League Final Eight").

The country is represented in association football by the [Andorra national football team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_national_football_team "Andorra national football team"). The team gained its first competitive win in a European Championship qualifier on 11 October 2019, against Moldova.[\[227\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-233) Football is governed in Andorra by the [Andorran Football Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorran_Football_Federation "Andorran Football Federation") – founded in 1994, it organizes the national competitions of association football ([Primera Divisió](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primera_Divisi%C3%B3 "Primera Divisió"), [Copa Constitució](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copa_Constituci%C3%B3 "Copa Constitució") and [Supercopa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorran_Supercup "Andorran Supercup")) and [futsal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futsal "Futsal"). Andorra was admitted to [UEFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA "UEFA") and [FIFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA "FIFA") in the same year, 1996. [FC Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FC_Andorra "FC Andorra"), a club based in [Andorra la Vella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_la_Vella "Andorra la Vella") founded in 1942, compete in the [Spanish football league system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_football_league_system "Spanish football league system").

[Rugby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby_union "Rugby union") is a traditional sport in Andorra, mainly influenced by its popularity in southern France. The [Andorra national rugby union team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andorra_national_rugby_union_team "Andorra national rugby union team"), nicknamed Els Isards, plays on the international stage in [rugby union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby_union "Rugby union") and [rugby sevens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby_sevens "Rugby sevens").[\[228\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-234) [VPC Andorra XV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VPC_Andorra_XV "VPC Andorra XV") is a rugby team based in Andorra la Vella, which actually plays in the French championship.

Basketball's popularity has increased in the country since the 1990s, when the Andorran team [BC Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BC_Andorra "BC Andorra") played in the top league of Spain ([Liga ACB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liga_ACB "Liga ACB")).[\[229\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-235) After 18 years the club returned to the top league in 2014.[\[230\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-236)

Other sports practised in Andorra include cycling, volleyball, judo, Australian Rules football, handball, swimming, gymnastics, tennis, and motorsports. In 2012, Andorra raised its first national cricket team and played a home match against the Dutch Fellowship of Fairly Odd Places Cricket Club, the first match played in the history of Andorra at an altitude of 1,300 metres (4,300 ft).[\[231\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-237)

Andorra first participated at the [Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_Games "Olympic Games") in 1976. The country has appeared in every [Winter Olympic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter_Olympic "Winter Olympic") Games since [1976](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1976_Winter_Olympics "1976 Winter Olympics"). Andorra competes in the [Games of the Small States of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Games_of_the_Small_States_of_Europe "Games of the Small States of Europe"), being twice the host country, in [1991](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1991_Games_of_the_Small_States_of_Europe "1991 Games of the Small States of Europe") and [2005](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_Games_of_the_Small_States_of_Europe "2005 Games of the Small States of Europe").

As one of the [Catalan Countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_Countries "Catalan Countries"), Andorra is home to a team of [castellers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castell "Castell"), or Catalan human tower builders. The [Castellers d'Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Castellers_d%27Andorra\&action=edit\&redlink=1 "Castellers d'Andorra (page does not exist)") \[[ca](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castellers_d%27Andorra "ca:Castellers d'Andorra")], based in the town of [Santa Coloma d'Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Coloma_d%27Andorra "Santa Coloma d'Andorra"), are recognized by the [Coordinadora de Colles Castelleres de Catalunya](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Coordinadora_de_Colles_Castelleres_de_Catalunya\&action=edit\&redlink=1 "Coordinadora de Colles Castelleres de Catalunya (page does not exist)") \[[ca](https://ca.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinadora_de_Colles_Castelleres_de_Catalunya "ca:Coordinadora de Colles Castelleres de Catalunya")], the governing body of castells.

## Tourism

\[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Andorra\&action=edit\&section=37 "Edit section: Tourism")]

Main article: [Tourism in Andorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_Andorra "Tourism in Andorra")

Skiing, hiking, cross-country running, and cycling are all popular sports tourism activities in Andorra.[\[232\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:0-238) Andorra's economy is heavily built on tourism.[\[232\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-:0-238)

Andorra generally enjoys a ski season from late November to early April, depending on weather conditions.[\[233\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-239) Outside of the ski season, some of the ski lift facilities continue to operate at lower capacity, facilitating access to popular view points such as the Tristaina solar viewpoint, a viewpoint–sundial located on the peak of Peyreguils at an altitude of 2,701 metres and a few metres from the geographical border between Andorra and France. This peak is part of the Tristaina cirque, along with the Costa Rodona, Tristaina, Creussans, and Cabanyó peaks.[\[234\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-240)
